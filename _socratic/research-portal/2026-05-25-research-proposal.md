# Enhancing Critical Thinking in Bangladesh’s Higher Secondary Education through Transgressive Pedagogy and Socratic 4.0: A Mixed-Methods Pilot Study

**Md. Yusuf** Lecturer, Department of Zoology, Government Syed Hatem Ali College, Barishal  
BCS Education Cadre Officer (38th BCS Education Cadre)

---

### Abstract
Bangladesh’s Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) education system continues to face substantial pedagogical challenges, including rote memorization, hierarchical classroom structures, limited student agency, and insufficient development of higher-order thinking skills. This research proposes a transformative pedagogical framework titled **Socratic 4.0**, which integrates transgressive pedagogy, the Socratic method, and Web 4.0 digital learning technologies to foster critical thinking, reflective learning, and student empowerment in Bangladeshi HSC classrooms.

---

## 1. Introduction & Problem Statement
The existing HSC education system in Bangladesh remains heavily dependent on rote memorization and teacher-centered instruction. In many classrooms, cultural hierarchies discourage questioning and independent thinking. Consequently, students frequently develop passive learning habits rather than analytical competencies.

This study proposes an innovative pedagogical framework termed **Socratic 4.0**, designed to contextualize the Socratic method within Bangladesh’s educational realities by integrating transgressive pedagogy, AI-assisted tools, and Multiple Intelligences (MI)-based learner exploration.

---

## 2. Methodological Framework
This study adopts a mixed-methods research design incorporating qualitative action research, quantitative pre/post critical thinking assessments, and multiple-case study approaches.



### Data Collection Methods:
* **Phase 1 (Development):** Building Socratic reflective questionnaires and MI-based inquiry tools.
* **Phase 2 (Implementation):** Conducting iterative questioning loops through WhatsApp groups, Padlet walls, and Google Forms branching structures.
* **Phase 3 (Evaluation):** Analyzing reflective journals, semi-structured interviews, and rubric-based evaluations.

---

## 3. Theoretical & Spiritual Convergence
Grounded in Paulo Freire’s critical pedagogy and bell hooks’ transgressive teaching philosophy, this study adapts dialogic learning approaches to the socio-cultural realities of Bangladesh. 

By utilizing dynamic, learner-centered exploration, Socratic 4.0 encourages students to analyze information critically. This intellectual awakening perfectly supports the practice of *Tafakkur* (deep contemplation), allowing students to connect academic discipline mechanics (like the biological systems of the body) to real-world behavioral choices and their ultimate spiritual purpose.
