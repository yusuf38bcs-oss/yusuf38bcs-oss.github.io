OBSERVE · A hypothetical experiment

A living plant cell is placed in a concentrated salt solution.

Assume the surrounding solution has lower water potential than the cell interior, and the plasma membrane is intact.

Predict the net movement of water.

Choose a prediction, then check your reasoning.

EVIDENCE · EXPLAIN · REVISE

Inspect the water-potential model

Expected direction: water leaves the cell. Water moves by osmosis from higher to lower water potential across a selectively permeable membrane.

If enough water leaves, the vacuole can shrink and the plasma membrane may pull away from the cell wall (plasmolysis). The extent depends on the gradient, exposure and membrane properties.

What would count as evidence? Compare cell images before and after exposure, document the change, and test a less concentrated solution as a comparison.
