Animal Diversity Matrix is the gateway for understanding the architecture of animal life. This node connects taxonomy, symmetry, germ layers, coelom, segmentation, larval forms, organ systems, and evolutionary adaptation into one navigable learning path.

## Learning Map

- **Classification logic:** body plan, level of organization, symmetry, coelom, segmentation, and notochord.
- **Invertebrate foundations:** Protozoa, Porifera, Cnidaria, Platyhelminthes, Nematoda, Annelida, Arthropoda, Mollusca, and Echinodermata.
- **Chordate transition:** Urochordata, Cephalochordata, Cyclostomata, fishes, amphibians, reptiles, birds, and mammals.
- **Applied focus:** adaptive radiation, parasitism, locomotion, reproduction, and ecological role.

## Study Sequence

1. Start with diagnostic characters of each phylum.
2. Compare body plans using symmetry, body cavity, segmentation, and organ-system complexity.
3. Link each structural innovation to survival advantage.
4. Finish with MCQ practice and short-answer recall.
