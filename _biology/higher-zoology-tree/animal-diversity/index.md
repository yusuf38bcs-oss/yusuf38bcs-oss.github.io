# Animal Diversity

Animal Diversity is a ten-lecture Higher Zoology course focused on chordate body plans, classification, structure–function relationships, adaptation, and representative systems from lower chordates to humans.

[Open the course index →]({{ '/biology/animal-diversity/course/' | relative_url }})

## Course scope

The sequence begins with the chordate plan and classification, then moves through *Ascidia*, *Branchiostoma*, cyclostomes, *Scoliodon*, *Labeo rohita*, *Bufo*, *Hemidactylus*, *Columba livia*, and the human eye and ear.
