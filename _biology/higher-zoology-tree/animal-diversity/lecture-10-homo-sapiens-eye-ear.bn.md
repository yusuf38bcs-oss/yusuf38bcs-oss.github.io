# Homo sapiens: Eye, Ear and Mammalian Diversity

### The Puzzle: perception organ-এ নয়, system-এ

Eye “দেখে” এবং ear “শোনে”—এই ভাষা convenient, কিন্তু physiologically incomplete।

বাস্তবে:

> **Sense organ physical energyকে neural signal-এ convert করে; brain সেই signal interpret করে perception তৈরি করে।**

এই lecture শেষে তুমি পারবে:

- Mammalia diagnostic features লিখতে;
- Homo sapiens systematic position দিতে;
- eye-এর three coats/chambers/optical pathway explain করতে;
- retina, rods, cones ও vision mechanism বুঝতে;
- accommodation, pupil reflex basics বলতে;
- ear-এর outer, middle, inner parts লিখতে;
- ossicles ও cochlea-এর hearing mechanism explain করতে;
- eye vs ear sensory transduction compare করতে;
- monotremes, marsupials, aquatic mammals, apes ও Bengal tiger brief notes লিখতে;
- ক/খ/গ answers তৈরি করতে।

## 1. Systematic Position of Homo sapiens

- **Phylum:** Chordata
- **Subphylum:** Vertebrata
- **Class:** Mammalia
- **Order:** Primates
- **Family:** Hominidae
- **Genus:** *Homo*
- **Species:** *Homo sapiens*

## 2. Mammalia Diagnostic Characters

- hair at least at some life stage;
- mammary glands;
- three middle-ear ossicles: malleus, incus, stapes;
- dentary-squamosal jaw joint;
- heterodont teeth common;
- muscular diaphragm;
- four-chambered heart;
- endothermy;
- well-developed neocortex;
- internal fertilization;
- parental care usually extensive।

#### Most diagnostic triad

**Hair + mammary glands + three middle-ear ossicles**

## Part I — Human Eye

## 4. Eye as an Optical + Neural Organ

Eye-এর কাজ তিন layer-এ ভাবো:

1. light capture;
2. optical focusing;
3. neural transduction।

![Human eye pathway](/assets/biology/animal-diversity/lecture-10/lecture-10-human-eye.svg)

---

## 5. Wall of the Eyeball: Three Coats

### 5.1 Fibrous coat

- sclera;
- cornea।

Cornea transparent and major refracting surface।

### 5.2 Vascular coat / uvea

- choroid;
- ciliary body;
- iris।

Iris pupil size control করে।

### 5.3 Neural coat

- retina।

Retina photoreceptors and neuronal layers contain করে।

---

## 6. Chambers and Media

### Aqueous humor
Cornea–lens anterior region-এর fluid; pressure, nutrition and optics-এ role।

### Lens
Flexible transparent structure; focus fine-tune করে।

### Vitreous humor
Posterior cavity-এর gel; eyeball shape support করে।

---

## 7. Optical Pathway

National Eye Institute summary অনুযায়ী:

```text
Light
 ↓
Cornea
 ↓
Pupil
 ↓
Lens
 ↓
Vitreous
 ↓
Retina
 ↓
Electrical signals
 ↓
Optic nerve
 ↓
Brain
```

Cornea + lens light retina-তে focus করে।

---

## 8. Iris and Pupil

Pupil hole; iris colored muscular diaphragm।

Bright light → pupil constricts।  
Dim light → pupil dilates।

This regulates retinal illumination।

---

## 9. Accommodation

Near/far focus adjust করতে ciliary muscle lens curvature change করে।

#### Near object

- ciliary muscle contracts;
- zonular tension decreases;
- lens more convex;
- refractive power increases।

#### Distant object

- ciliary muscle relaxes;
- zonular tension increases;
- lens flatter।

---

## 10. Retina

Retina major neural layers:

- photoreceptors;
- bipolar cells;
- ganglion cells;
- horizontal/amacrine interneurons।

Simplified signal route:

```text
Rods / cones
   ↓
Bipolar cells
   ↓
Ganglion cells
   ↓
Optic nerve
```

---

## 11. Rods and Cones

### Rods

- high sensitivity;
- dim-light vision;
- low color discrimination;
- peripheral retina abundant।

### Cones

- bright-light vision;
- color;
- high acuity;
- fovea-rich।

#### Fovea

Central high-acuity region with dense cones।

---

## 12. Blind Spot

Optic disc:

- ganglion axons leave eye;
- no photoreceptors;
- physiological blind spot।

Brain perceptually fills missing region using binocular/context information।

---

## 13. Vision Mechanism

1. light enters cornea;
2. pupil regulates quantity;
3. lens focuses;
4. retinal image forms inverted optically;
5. photoreceptors absorb photons;
6. phototransduction changes membrane signaling;
7. retinal circuits process contrast/color/spatial information;
8. ganglion cell action potentials leave via optic nerve;
9. visual pathways reach brain;
10. cortical processing produces perception।

#### Important correction

Brain does not simply “turn upside-down image upright” like rotating a photograph। Perception emerges from neural spatial processing।

## Part II — Human Ear

## 14. Three Divisions

1. outer ear;
2. middle ear;
3. inner ear।

![Human ear pathway](/assets/biology/animal-diversity/lecture-10/lecture-10-human-ear.svg)

---

## 15. Outer Ear

- pinna/auricle;
- external auditory canal;
- tympanic membrane boundary।

Function: sound collect/direct to eardrum।

---

## 16. Middle Ear

Contains three ossicles:

1. malleus;
2. incus;
3. stapes।

#### Why three ossicles matter?

They transfer and amplify/match vibration from air-filled outer ear to fluid-filled cochlea।

Stapes footplate acts on oval window।

#### Eustachian tube

Middle-ear pressure equalization with pharynx।

---

## 17. Inner Ear

Major systems:

- cochlea — hearing;
- vestibule — balance;
- semicircular canals — angular acceleration।

---

## 18. Cochlea

Fluid-filled spiral structure।

Important internal elements:

- scala vestibuli;
- scala media;
- scala tympani;
- basilar membrane;
- organ of Corti;
- hair cells;
- tectorial membrane।

---

## 19. Organ of Corti

Sensory epithelium on basilar membrane।

Hair-cell stereocilia deflection mechanical energy → receptor electrical signal convert করে।

---

## 20. Hearing Mechanism

NIDCD stepwise framework:

```text
Sound wave
 ↓
Ear canal
 ↓
Tympanic membrane vibrates
 ↓
Malleus → incus → stapes
 ↓
Oval window
 ↓
Cochlear fluid wave
 ↓
Basilar membrane motion
 ↓
Hair-cell stereocilia bend
 ↓
Electrical signal
 ↓
Cochlear nerve
 ↓
Brain
```

---

## 21. Pitch Coding

Basilar membrane tonotopic।

- base region stiffer/narrower → high frequencies;
- apical region more flexible → low frequencies।

Thus frequency represented spatially along cochlea।

---

## 22. Balance

### Semicircular canals
Angular rotation detect করে।

### Utricle/saccule
Linear acceleration এবং head tilt detect করে।

Hair cells again mechanotransduction use করে।

---

## 23. Eye vs Ear: Common Principle

![Eye and ear comparison](/assets/biology/animal-diversity/lecture-10/lecture-10-eye-ear-comparison.svg)

| Eye | Ear |
|---|---|
| stimulus: light | stimulus: sound/mechanical motion |
| receptor: rods/cones | receptor: hair cells |
| transduction: photon → electrical | stereocilia deflection → electrical |
| nerve: optic | cochlear/vestibular |
| brain interprets vision | brain interprets sound/balance |

Core rule:

> **Different physical energies, same systems logic: transduction → coding → neural interpretation.**

## 24. Mammalian Diversity

![Mammalian diversity](/assets/biology/animal-diversity/lecture-10/lecture-10-mammal-diversity.svg)

### Monotremes

Egg-laying mammals।

Examples:
- platypus;
- echidnas।

Mammary glands আছে, nipples typical নেই; milk skin/fur-associated openings থেকে delivered।

### Marsupials

Short gestation; young highly altricial; pouch common but not universal in identical form।

Examples:
- kangaroo;
- opossum।

### Placentals

Placenta-mediated prolonged intrauterine development typical।

---

## 25. Aquatic Mammals

Examples:

- whales/dolphins;
- seals;
- manatees।

Despite aquatic shape:

- lungs;
- mammary glands;
- hair at some stage;
- three ear ossicles;
- mammalian reproduction।

#### Convergence lesson

Whale fish নয়। Streamlining analogous environmental adaptation।

---

## 26. Apes

Hominoidea includes gibbons and great apes।

General traits:

- no external tail;
- mobile shoulder;
- large brain relative to body;
- complex social/behavioral systems।

Humans are apes in modern taxonomy—family Hominidae, genus *Homo*।

---

## 27. Bengal Tiger

*Panthera tigris tigris*।

Mammalian diagnostic features plus carnivoran specializations:

- heterodont teeth;
- large canines/carnassials;
- retractile claws;
- predatory sensory system।

Bangladesh ecological relevance: Sundarbans population iconic, conservation context important।

## 28. ক-বিভাগ — Exam Capsule

### Retina কী?
Eye-এর light-sensitive neural layer containing photoreceptors and retinal neurons।

### Optic nerve কী?
Retinal ganglion-cell axons carrying visual signals toward brain।

### Auditory ossicles-এর নাম?
Malleus, incus, stapes।

### Organ of Corti কী?
Cochlea-এর basilar membrane-এর উপর hearing sensory epithelium।

### Diastema কী?
Teeth-এর মধ্যে toothless gap; many herbivorous mammals-এ prominent।

---

## 29. খ-বিভাগ — Vision Mechanism

Short answer:

Cornea/lens light focus করে retina-তে। Rods/cones lightকে neural signals-এ transduce করে। Bipolar/ganglion cells process করে; optic nerve brain-এ signal বহন করে। Visual cortex interpretation-এর মাধ্যমে perception তৈরি করে।

---

## 30. খ-বিভাগ — Hearing Mechanism

Sound → tympanum → ossicles → oval window → cochlear fluid → basilar membrane → hair cells → cochlear nerve → brain।

---

- choroid/ciliary body/iris;
- retina;

- aqueous/vitreous;
- optic nerve।

#### Mechanism
refraction → focus → phototransduction → retinal processing → optic nerve → brain।

## 32. গ-বিভাগ — Model Long Answer 2
### “মানবকর্ণের গঠন ও hearing mechanism বর্ণনা কর।”

#### Outer ear
pinna + canal।

#### Middle ear

## 33. Common Mistakes

1. Pupilকে black tissue বলা—it's an opening।
2. Lens main light-sensitive organ বলা।
3. Retina “image stores” লেখা।
4. Stapes round window-এ attaches লেখা—primarily oval window।
5. Semicircular canals main hearing organ বলা।
6. Hair cells regenerate freely in adult humans বলা।
7. Whaleকে fish বা platypusকে non-mammal বলা।

| System | Key sequence |

| hearing | canal → eardrum → ossicles → cochlea → nerve → brain |

## 35. Self-Test

<details><summary><strong>Why is retina nervous tissue rather than a simple screen?</strong></summary>
It contains photoreceptors and multiple neural layers that transduce and preprocess visual information before signals leave through the optic nerve.
</details>

<details><summary><strong>Why are ossicles necessary?</strong></summary>
They improve transfer of vibration from low-impedance air to high-impedance cochlear fluid and mechanically couple eardrum motion to the oval window.
</details>

<details><summary><strong>Why is a whale a mammal?</strong></summary>
Mammary glands, lungs, hair at some life stage, mammalian ear/jaw anatomy and reproduction provide deeper diagnostic evidence than aquatic body shape.
</details>

## 36. Transfer Challenge

একটি animal egg lays, but has hair, mammary glands and three middle-ear ossicles।

**Diagnosis:** Mammalia—specifically monotreme-type mammal। Egg laying alone does not define Reptilia/Aves।

## 37. Research-backed Update Notes

- Mammalian classification should rely on diagnostic anatomy, not reproductive mode alone.
- “Aquatic = fish” is a classic convergence error।
