# Bufo

### The Puzzle: land-এ ওঠা, কিন্তু water থেকে পুরো মুক্ত নয়

Toad land-এ locomotion করে, lungs ব্যবহার করে, eyelids আছে, limbs আছে। তবু skin moist রাখতে হয়, eggs shell-less, tadpole aquatic।

Amphibia তাই “অসম্পূর্ণ reptile” নয়। এটি একটি distinctive life strategy:

> **Terrestrial adult + water-dependent reproduction and moist physiological interface**

এই lecture শেষে তুমি পারবে:

- Bufo systematic position ও diagnostic features লিখতে;
- amphibian water–land compromises explain করতে;
- respiration-এর cutaneous, buccopharyngeal ও pulmonary routes বুঝতে;
- heart ও double circulation outline করতে;
- hepatic ও renal portal systems trace করতে;
- reproduction, tadpole ও metamorphosis লিখতে;
- living Amphibia classification এবং adaptive radiation বুঝতে;
- ক/খ/গ answer লিখতে।

---

## 1. Systematic Position

Traditional teaching genus **Bufo** broadly refers to true toads; South Asian “common toad” treatments historically used *Bufo melanostictus*, now commonly placed as *Duttaphrynus melanostictus*।

NU-friendly framework:

- **Phylum:** Chordata
- **Subphylum:** Vertebrata
- **Class:** Amphibia
- **Order:** Anura
- **Family:** Bufonidae
- **Traditional genus:** *Bufo*

#### Taxonomy note

Exam-এ syllabus term Bufo ব্যবহার করবে; modern accepted species name source অনুযায়ী আলাদা note করতে পারো।

---

## 2. Amphibia Diagnostic Characters

- ectothermic tetrapods;
- skin thin, moist, glandular;
- scales usually absent in frogs/toads;
- two pairs limbs in typical forms;
- lungs in adult but cutaneous respiration important;
- three-chambered heart;
- eggs usually shell-less/anamniotic;
- fertilization often external in anurans;
- larva often aquatic;
- metamorphosis common।

---

## 3. External Morphology

Adult toad:

- head and trunk; neck short;
- tail absent;
- forelimbs shorter, hindlimbs stronger;
- eyes with eyelids/nictitating membrane;
- external tympanum;
- nostrils;
- wide mouth;
- parotoid glands in many toads;
- rough/warty glandular skin।

![Bufo body plan](/assets/biology/animal-diversity/lecture-07/lecture-07-bufo-body-plan.svg)

---

## 4. Skin: শুধু covering নয়, physiological organ

Amphibian skin:

- mucus glands;
- poison/granular glands;
- rich capillary supply;
- water exchange;
- gas exchange।

#### Why must it remain moist?

Gas diffusion membrane-এর জন্য dissolved gases needed। Dry skin cutaneous respiration reduce করে।

---

## 5. Skeleton and Locomotion

Adaptations to land:

- strong pectoral/pelvic girdles;
- limbs support body;
- elongated hindlimbs jumping/hopping;
- fused/modified bones reduce mass and handle impact;
- vertebral column stronger than fish।

Toads generally short powerful hops/walks rather than frog-like long jumps।

---

## 6. Feeding

Adult toads carnivorous/insectivorous।

Mechanism:

- visual prey detection;
- protrusible sticky tongue;
- rapid tongue projection;
- prey swallowed mostly whole।

Teeth absent/reduced in many true toads compared with frogs।

---

## 7. Digestive System

```text
Mouth → buccopharyngeal cavity → esophagus → stomach
→ small intestine → large intestine → cloaca
```

Associated organs:

- liver;
- gall bladder;
- pancreas।

Cloaca digestive, urinary and reproductive outflow-এর common chamber।

---

## 8. Respiration

Adult amphibian একাধিক route ব্যবহার করে।

### 8.1 Cutaneous respiration

Moist vascular skin দিয়ে O₂/CO₂ diffusion।

Important during:

- rest;
- submergence;
- winter dormancy।

### 8.2 Buccopharyngeal respiration

Mouth floor movements buccopharyngeal lining-এর উপর air renew করে।

### 8.3 Pulmonary respiration

Lungs simple sac-like।

Air movement positive-pressure buccal pump দ্বারা:

```text
Nostrils open
 ↓
Buccal cavity fills
 ↓
Glottis opens
 ↓
Buccal floor rises
 ↓
Air forced into lungs
```

Mammalian negative-pressure diaphragm system নেই।

---

## 9. Heart and Double Circulation

Heart:

- sinus venosus;
- right atrium;
- left atrium;
- single ventricle;
- conus/truncus region।

#### Two circuits

- pulmocutaneous circuit;
- systemic circuit।

Complete separation না হলেও cardiac architecture oxygenated/deoxygenated bloodকে partially segregate করে।

---

## 10. Portal System কী?

Portal system-এ blood:

> **এক capillary bed → vein → আরেক capillary bed**

অর্থাৎ vein সরাসরি heart-এ না গিয়ে second capillary network-এ blood নিয়ে যায়।

Bufo-তে দুইটি major portal system exam-important:

1. hepatic portal;
2. renal portal।

![Bufo portal systems](/assets/biology/animal-diversity/lecture-07/lecture-07-bufo-portal-system.svg)

---

## 11. Hepatic Portal System

Digestive tract থেকে nutrient-rich blood liver-এ আসে।

Simplified pathway:

```text
Stomach/intestine/spleen
      ↓
Hepatic portal vein
      ↓
Liver sinusoids
      ↓
Hepatic veins
      ↓
Postcaval vein
      ↓
Sinus venosus / heart
```

#### Functional significance

Liver:

- absorbed nutrients process করে;
- glucose metabolism;
- detoxification;
- storage;
- metabolic regulation।

So intestine → liver first passage physiologically logical।

---

## 12. Renal Portal System

Posterior body/hindlimb venous blood-এর একটি portion kidney capillaries-এর দিকে route হতে পারে।

Simplified:

```text
Hindlimb/posterior body
     ↓
Renal portal veins
     ↓
Kidney capillary network
     ↓
Renal veins
     ↓
Postcaval circulation
```

Bullfrog vascular-cast studies renal portal input into kidney microcirculation confirm করে।

#### Important correction

Renal portal system মানে “all hindlimb blood must go only to kidneys” নয়। Venous connections/shunts variation থাকতে পারে।

---

## 13. Why Portal Systems Matter

Portal design allows organ-specific processing before blood returns to heart।

- hepatic portal → nutrient processing;
- renal portal → kidney perfusion/filter-regulation pathways।

This is a circulation architecture question, শুধু vein names নয়।

---

## 14. Excretion

Adult amphibians:

- kidneys mesonephric/opisthonephric type in classical terminology;
- ureters;
- urinary bladder;
- cloaca।

Adults often ureotelic—urea major nitrogenous waste।

Aquatic larvae more ammonotelic হতে পারে।

---

## 15. Nervous and Sensory Systems

- differentiated brain;
- cranial nerves;
- eyes with eyelids;
- tympanic membrane + middle ear;
- olfaction;
- balance system।

Tympanum airborne sound detection-এ terrestrial adaptation।

---

## 16. Reproduction

Anurans typically breeding water-এ।

- sexes separate;
- male amplexus;
- eggs released in water;
- external fertilization common;
- jelly-coated eggs;
- larval tadpole।

#### Why water dependence remains?

Egg lacks amniotic shell and desiccates easily; sperm transfer/fertilization and larval gill-based development water-dependent।

---

## 17. Tadpole and Metamorphosis

Tadpole:

- aquatic;
- tail;
- gills;
- lateral-line system;
- plant/detrital feeding common;
- initially fish-like locomotion।

Metamorphosis:

- limbs develop;
- tail resorbs;
- gills disappear;
- lungs become dominant;
- gut shortens/remodels for carnivory;
- skull and sensory systems reorganize।

Thyroid hormone metamorphosis regulate করে।

---

## 18. Living Amphibia Classification

![Living Amphibia classification](/assets/biology/animal-diversity/lecture-07/lecture-07-amphibia-classification.svg)

### Anura
Frogs and toads; adult tail absent।

### Caudata/Urodela
Salamanders and newts; adult tail present।

### Gymnophiona/Apoda
Caecilians; limbless burrowing amphibians।

---

## 19. Adaptive Radiation of Amphibia

Adaptive radiation মানে different ecological niches-এ structural/functional diversification।

Examples:

- aquatic frogs;
- terrestrial toads;
- arboreal tree frogs;
- burrowing caecilians;
- aquatic/terrestrial salamanders;
- neotenic salamanders retaining larval traits।

#### Important point

Adaptive radiation = “higher to lower” ranking নয়; ecological diversification।

---

## 20. Neoteny

**Neoteny** হলো reproductive maturity অর্জনের পরও juvenile/larval characters retain করা।

Example: axolotl-type salamander systems।

Amphibian developmental plasticity বোঝাতে important।

---

## 21. ক-বিভাগ — Exam Capsule

### Portal system কী?
এক capillary bed থেকে venous blood second capillary bed-এ যাওয়ার vascular arrangement।

### Aestivation কী?
Hot/dry unfavorable season-এ reduced activity/dormancy।

### Neoteny কী?
Sexual maturity অর্জন করেও larval/juvenile characters retain করা।

### Tympanum কী?
External membrane transmitting airborne vibration to middle ear।

### Amplexus কী?
Anuran breeding embrace facilitating external fertilization।

---

## 22. খ-বিভাগ — Model Answer
### Hepatic portal এবং renal portal system-এর পার্থক্য

| Feature | Hepatic portal | Renal portal |
|---|---|---|
| origin | digestive organs | hindlimb/posterior body |
| portal vein | hepatic portal vein | renal portal veins |
| second capillary bed | liver sinusoids | kidney capillaries |
| major role | nutrient/metabolic processing | renal perfusion/excretory regulation |

---

## 23. গ-বিভাগ — Model Long Answer
### “Bufo-এর portal system বর্ণনা কর।”

#### Definition
Portal circulation second capillary bed-এ venous blood পাঠায়।

#### Hepatic portal
digestive organs → hepatic portal vein → liver sinusoids → hepatic veins → postcaval system।

#### Renal portal
posterior body/hindlimbs → renal portal veins → kidney capillary network → renal veins/postcaval।

#### Significance
Organ-specific processing before return to heart।

#### Diagram
Two systems side-by-side।

Start with general characters, then:

- Anura;
- Caudata/Urodela;
- Gymnophiona/Apoda;

for each give 2–3 diagnostic characters + example।

---

## 25. Common Mistakes

1. Amphibian lungs mammalian alveolar lungs-এর মতো লেখা।
2. Renal portal = kidney artery বলা।
3. Hepatic portal vein oxygenated artery বলা।
4. Tadpole-এ lungs only respiration লেখা।
5. Amphibian egg amniotic shell আছে লেখা।
6. Caecilianকে snake/reptile বলা।

## 27. Self-Test

<details><summary><strong>Why is hepatic portal circulation useful?</strong></summary>
Nutrient-rich blood from the gut reaches the liver first, allowing metabolic processing, storage and detoxification before systemic return.
</details>

<details><summary><strong>Why is Bufo not fully independent of water?</strong></summary>
Its moist skin and, especially, shell-less eggs and aquatic larval development preserve strong dependence on water/moist environments.
</details>

---

## 28. Transfer Challenge

একটি tetrapod has lungs, limbs and tympanum, but lays shell-less eggs in water and has an aquatic gilled larva।

**Diagnosis:** Amphibia pattern, not reptilian amniote pattern।

---

## 29. Research-backed Update Notes

- Modern amphibian taxonomy recognizes Anura, Caudata and Gymnophiona as the three living orders.
- Renal portal blood supply in frogs is anatomically documented.
- Traditional *Bufo melanostictus* nomenclature has changed in modern taxonomy; retain syllabus terminology for exam alignment and note accepted names separately.
