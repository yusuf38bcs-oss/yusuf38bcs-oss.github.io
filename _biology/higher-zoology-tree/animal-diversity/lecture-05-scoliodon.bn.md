# Scoliodon

### The Puzzle: bone না থাকলেও “less advanced” নয়

*Scoliodon* একটি small requiem shark। Skeleton cartilage-এর, swim bladder নেই, কিন্তু body অত্যন্ত efficient hydrodynamic machine। Internal fertilization, placoid scales, sophisticated sensory systems এবং urea-based osmoregulation দেখায় যে cartilage মানেই “simple” নয়।

এই lecture শেষে তুমি পারবে:

- *Scoliodon* systematic position ও body plan লিখতে;
- Chondrichthyes diagnostic characters explain করতে;
- placoid scale, gills, circulation ও osmoregulation relate করতে;
- male ও female urinogenital systems আলাদা বুঝতে;
- claspers ও internal fertilization explain করতে;
- sharks, skates, rays compare করতে;
- Chondrichthyes classification-এর modern এবং exam-friendly framework আলাদা রাখতে;
- ক/খ/গ model answer লিখতে।

---

## 1. Systematic Position

Current placement of *Scoliodon laticaudus*:

- **Phylum:** Chordata
- **Subphylum:** Vertebrata
- **Class:** Chondrichthyes
- **Subclass:** Elasmobranchii
- **Order:** Carcharhiniformes
- **Family:** Carcharhinidae
- **Genus:** *Scoliodon*

FishBase current classificationও *Scoliodon laticaudus*কে Elasmobranchii → Carcharhiniformes → Carcharhinidae-তে রাখে।

---

## 2. Chondrichthyes Diagnostic Characters

- endoskeleton predominantly cartilage;
- jaws present;
- paired pectoral ও pelvic fins;
- placoid scales common;
- 5–7 pairs exposed gill slits in many elasmobranchs;
- operculum absent in typical sharks;
- swim bladder absent;
- heterocercal tail common in sharks;
- internal fertilization;
- male pelvic fins form claspers;
- spiral valve intestine common;
- rectal gland participates in salt balance;
- high urea/TMAO strategy aids osmotic balance।

---

## 3. External Morphology

Scoliodon body:

- spindle-shaped;
- head, trunk, tail regions;
- ventral mouth;
- paired nostrils;
- eyes;
- spiracle small/variable in many carcharhinids;
- five gill slits;
- pectoral and pelvic fins;
- dorsal fins;
- heterocercal caudal fin;
- male claspers at pelvic fins।

## 4. Placoid Scale

Placoid scales বা dermal denticles shark skin-এর characteristic।

Each has:

- basal plate in dermis;
- dentine body;
- enamel/enameloid-like outer covering;
- backward-directed spine।

#### Functions

- protection;
- friction/drag modification;
- hydrodynamic flow control।

#### Affinity with teeth

Placoid denticles এবং vertebrate teeth-এর developmental/material similarities important evolutionary comparison।

---

## 5. Skeleton

Cartilage-based endoskeleton।

Main divisions:

- axial skeleton;
- cranium/chondrocranium;
- vertebral column;
- visceral arches;
- paired girdles and fins।

#### Chondrocranium

Brain ও sense capsulesকে protect/support করা cartilaginous skull structure।

---

## 6. Digestive System

Path:

```text
Mouth → buccal cavity → pharynx → esophagus → stomach
→ intestine with spiral valve → rectum → cloaca
```

#### Spiral valve

Intestinal surface area increase করে without extremely long intestine।

#### Liver

Large, oil-rich liver:

- metabolism;
- buoyancy assistance;
- energy storage।

Swim bladder না থাকায় buoyancy control-এ liver oil, dynamic lift এবং body form গুরুত্বপূর্ণ।

---

## 7. Respiration

Water mouth/spiracle region দিয়ে pharynx-এ আসে, gill lamellae পার হয়ে external gill slits দিয়ে বের হয়।

Gas exchange:

- secondary lamellar surfaces;
- blood-water gradient;
- branchial circulation।

Active swimming অনেক shark species-এ ventilation enhance করে; species-specific ram ventilation/active buccal pumping variation থাকে।

---

## 8. Circulation

Single circulation:

```text
Sinus venosus
 → atrium
 → ventricle
 → conus arteriosus
 → ventral aorta
 → gills
 → dorsal aorta
 → body
 → veins
 → sinus venosus
```

Heart deoxygenated blood receive/pump করে; oxygenation gills-এ হয়।

---

## 9. Osmoregulation

Marine shark seawater-এর সঙ্গে osmotic challenge face করে।

Key strategy:

- blood/body fluids-এ urea high;
- TMAO proteinsকে urea-এর destabilizing effect থেকে protect করতে সাহায্য করে;
- rectal gland excess NaCl secrete করে;
- kidneys urea retain ও ions/water balance regulate করে।

#### Important concept

Shark seawater “drink করে freshwater fish-এর মতো” simple model নয়; osmotic strategy fundamentally different।

---

## 10. Urinogenital System Overview

“Urinogenital” কারণ excretory এবং reproductive ducts developmental/anatomicalভাবে close relationship দেখায়।

Adult kidney classical description-এ **opisthonephric**।

![Scoliodon urinogenital system](/assets/biology/animal-diversity/lecture-05/lecture-05-scoliodon-urinogenital.svg)

---

## 11. Male Urinogenital System

### Excretory part

- paired elongated kidneys;
- posterior renal region functional;
- ureters/urinary ducts urine transport করে;
- urinary/urinogenital sinus cloacal region-এর সঙ্গে connected।

### Reproductive part

- paired testes;
- vasa efferentia kidney region-এর সঙ্গে connect;
- mesonephric/Wolffian duct sperm transport pathway-এ modified;
- posterior region seminal vesicle-like expansion করতে পারে;
- sperm cloaca-র দিকে যায়।

### Claspers

Male pelvic fins-এর medial extensions।

Function:

- sperm transfer;
- internal fertilization।

---

## 12. Female Urinogenital System

### Excretory

Kidneys ও ureters urinary drainage handle করে।

### Reproductive

- paired ovaries (functional degree speciesভেদে variation);
- oviducal funnels;
- oviducts/Müllerian ducts;
- shell/oviducal gland;
- uterus-like posterior expansion in viviparous sharks;
- cloacal opening।

#### Scoliodon reproduction

Current FishBase information অনুযায়ী *Scoliodon laticaudus* viviparous and has placental nutrient transfer।

এটি classical “ovoviviparous shark” generalization থেকে more specific modern update।

---

## 13. Male vs Female Duct Logic

#### Male

Mesonephric/Wolffian duct reproductive transport-এ important।

#### Female

Genital duct (Müllerian/oviduct) urinary duct থেকে separate।

#### Exam memory

```text
Male: kidney + reproductive duct relation closer
Female: urinary and genital ducts more clearly separate
```

---

## 14. Nervous and Sensory Systems

Scoliodon-এ:

- well-developed brain;
- cranial nerves;
- olfactory organs;
- lateral line;
- inner ear;
- electroreceptive ampullae of Lorenzini in sharks।

#### Lateral line

Water displacement/vibration detect করে।

#### Ampullae

Weak electric fields and environmental cues detect করতে সাহায্য করে।

---

## 15. Reproduction and Development

- sexes separate;
- internal fertilization;
- claspers sperm transfer করে;
- embryos develop internally in many species;
- reproductive mode oviparity, ovoviviparity/lecithotrophy, viviparity—Chondrichthyes-এ diverse।

*Scoliodon laticaudus* modern sources-এ placental viviparity reported।

---

## 16. Sharks, Skates and Rays

### Sharks

- fusiform body;
- pectoral fins not fused with head;
- gill slits lateral;
- many active swimmers।

### Rays

- dorsoventrally flattened;
- enlarged pectoral fins fused with head;
- gill slits ventral;
- many benthic।

### Skates

Rays-এর মতো flattened batoids; many skates oviparous এবং egg cases (“mermaid’s purses”) produce করে।

#### Exam point

Skates vs rays পার্থক্য শুধু “size” নয়; tail, reproduction, electric/stinging structures, body form ইত্যাদি multiple features compare করো।

## 17. Chondrichthyes Classification

Modern higher-level map:

![Chondrichthyes classification](/assets/biology/animal-diversity/lecture-05/lecture-05-chondrichthyes-classification.svg)

- **Elasmobranchii** — sharks, skates, rays
- **Holocephali** — chimaeras; living order Chimaeriformes

#### Exam warning

Older Indian/Bangladesh zoology texts Chondrichthyes classification-এ different subclasses/orders names ব্যবহার করতে পারে। NU প্রশ্নে “up to order” চাইলে departmental prescribed scheme follow করা safest। Modern scheme note আলাদা দেওয়া যায়।

## 18. ক-বিভাগ — Exam Capsule

### Placoid scale কী?
Shark skin-এর tooth-like dermal denticle, with basal plate, dentine and an external spine।

### Clasper কী?
Male shark pelvic fin-এর modified copulatory organ, used in sperm transfer।

### Spiral valve কী?
Shark intestine-এর spiral internal fold, which increases absorptive surface area।

### Chondrocranium কী?
Primary cartilaginous skull surrounding brain and sensory capsules।

### Rectal gland-এর কাজ?
Excess salt excretion and osmoregulation।

---

## 19. খ-বিভাগ — Model Answer
### Skates ও Rays-এর পার্থক্য

Mention:

- both flattened batoids;
- skate tail usually thicker, without strong sting;
- many rays have whip-like tail and may bear stinging spine;
- skates commonly oviparous;
- many rays viviparous;
- electric organs occur in specific ray lineages, not defining all rays।

---

## 20. গ-বিভাগ — Model Long Answer
### “Scoliodon-এর urinogenital system বর্ণনা কর।”

#### Introduction
Excretory and reproductive systems are anatomically related, especially in males।

#### Male
- paired opisthonephric kidneys;
- urinary ducts;
- testes;
- vasa efferentia;
- Wolffian/mesonephric duct as sperm duct;
- posterior storage/duct regions;
- cloaca;
- claspers for transfer।

#### Female
- kidneys and ureters;
- ovaries;
- oviducal funnels;
- Müllerian ducts;
- shell glands;
- uterine regions;
- cloacal opening।

#### Conclusion
Male mesonephric ducts are strongly integrated with reproduction, while female urinary and genital pathways are more separate।

#### Diagram
Male and female separately।

## 22. Common Mistakes

1. Scoliodon skeleton “bone” লেখা।
2. Swim bladder আছে লেখা।
3. Clasper female organ বলা।
4. All sharks oviparous বলা।
5. Scoliodonকে universally ovoviviparous লেখা without species-specific update।
6. Rectal glandকে excretory kidney বলা।
7. Placoid scale = cycloid scale লেখা।

## 24. Self-Test

<details><summary><strong>Why can a shark remain buoyant without a swim bladder?</strong></summary>
Large oil-rich liver, hydrodynamic lift from fins/body, and active swimming combine to support buoyancy.
</details>

<details><summary><strong>Why is the term urinogenital especially useful in male sharks?</strong></summary>
Because kidney-associated mesonephric duct pathways are closely modified for sperm transport as well as excretory organization.
</details>

## 25. Transfer Challenge

একটি marine fish has placoid scales, claspers, 5 exposed gill slits, no swim bladder, and a cartilaginous skeleton।

**Diagnosis:** Chondrichthyes, most likely elasmobranch। These features outweigh general “fish-like” appearance।

---

## 26. Research-backed Update Notes

- FishBase places *Scoliodon laticaudus* in Carcharhiniformes, Carcharhinidae.
- Modern species accounts report placental viviparity in *S. laticaudus*.
- Elasmobranch osmoregulation depends on urea retention, TMAO and specialized salt secretion.
- Chondrichthyan reproductive modes are diverse; avoid one-rule generalization।
