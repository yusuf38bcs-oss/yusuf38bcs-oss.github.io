# Protozoa: Nutrition & Locomotion

## Concept Overview

**Protozoa** হলো unicellular eukaryotic animal-like protists. একক কোষ হয়েও তারা food intake, digestion, movement, irritability, reproduction and survival response পরিচালনা করে। Protozoa বুঝতে হলে দুটি প্রশ্ন সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ:

1. একক কোষ কীভাবে খাদ্য গ্রহণ করে?
2. একক কোষ কীভাবে চলাচল করে?

Nutrition and locomotion হলো protozoan survival-এর দুইটি কেন্দ্রীয় mechanism। খাদ্য ছাড়া energy নেই; movement ছাড়া food search, escape, host finding, light response বা habitat selection সম্ভব নয়।

## Why This Matters

Protozoa ছোট হলেও biological engineering-এর অসাধারণ উদাহরণ। Amoeba pseudopodia দিয়ে food engulf করে; Paramecium cilia দিয়ে food current তৈরি করে; Euglena flagellum দিয়ে চলে এবং light-sensitive response দেখায়; parasitic protozoa host tissue বা body fluid থেকে nutrient নেয়। তাই Protozoa lesson শুধু classification নয়; এটি cell-level survival strategy বোঝার foundation.

## Main Types of Protozoan Nutrition

<div class="lbfl-info-grid">
  <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Holozoic nutrition</h3><p>Solid food particles engulfed and digested inside food vacuoles.</p><p><strong>Example:</strong> Amoeba.</p></section>
  <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Holophytic nutrition</h3><p>Photosynthetic nutrition using chlorophyll-like pigments.</p><p><strong>Example:</strong> Euglena under light.</p></section>
  <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Saprozoic nutrition</h3><p>Dissolved organic substances absorbed through body surface.</p><p><strong>Example:</strong> some parasitic or free-living forms.</p></section>
  <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Parasitic nutrition</h3><p>Nutrient obtained from host body fluid, tissue, or intestinal content.</p><p><strong>Example:</strong> Entamoeba, Plasmodium-type parasitic forms.</p></section>
  <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Mixotrophic nutrition</h3><p>More than one nutritional mode depending on environment.</p><p><strong>Example:</strong> Euglena can photosynthesize and absorb nutrients.</p></section>
</div>

## Holozoic Nutrition in Amoeba

```text
Food particle detected
  ↓
Pseudopodia surround food
  ↓
Food vacuole forms
  ↓
Digestive enzymes act
  ↓
Useful nutrients absorbed into cytoplasm
  ↓
Undigested residue removed
```

## Ciliary Feeding in Paramecium

Paramecium uses cilia not only for movement but also for feeding. Cilia create a current that directs food particles toward the oral groove and cytostome. Then food vacuole forms and digestion occurs.

```text
Ciliary beating
  ↓
Food current forms
  ↓
Food enters oral groove
  ↓
Cytostome and cytopharynx
  ↓
Food vacuole
  ↓
Digestion and absorption
```

## Main Types of Protozoan Locomotion

<div class="lbfl-info-grid">
  <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Pseudopodial movement</h3><p>Temporary cytoplasmic projections move the body and engulf food.</p><p><strong>Example:</strong> Amoeba.</p></section>
  <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Flagellar movement</h3><p>One or more whip-like flagella create propulsion.</p><p><strong>Example:</strong> Euglena, Trypanosoma-like forms.</p></section>
  <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Ciliary movement</h3><p>Many short cilia beat rhythmically for movement and feeding current.</p><p><strong>Example:</strong> Paramecium.</p></section>
  <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Gliding movement</h3><p>Slow surface-associated movement without obvious cilia or flagella.</p><p><strong>Example:</strong> some sporozoan forms.</p></section>
</div>

## Pseudopodial Movement Logic

```text
Cytoplasm flows forward
  ↓
Temporary projection forms
  ↓
Cell body follows projection
  ↓
Direction changes according to stimulus
```

Pseudopodia are useful because they serve two functions at once: locomotion and food capture.

## Flagellar Movement Logic

Flagellum works like a whip-like or undulating propeller. Its movement pushes or pulls the organism through water. In Euglena, flagellum and eyespot/light response help the organism orient toward suitable light.

## Ciliary Movement Logic

Cilia beat in coordinated waves. Effective stroke pushes water backward or sideways; recovery stroke resets the cilium. Thousands of cilia together create rapid movement and feeding current.

```text
Effective stroke
  ↓
Water displacement
  ↓
Body moves / food current forms
  ↓
Recovery stroke
  ↓
Cycle repeats
```

## Nutrition vs Locomotion Comparison

| Topic | Main structure | Main purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Holozoic nutrition | pseudopodia / oral groove | solid food intake | Amoeba, Paramecium |
| Holophytic nutrition | chloroplast-like pigment | photosynthesis | Euglena |
| Saprozoic nutrition | body surface | dissolved nutrient absorption | some protozoa |
| Pseudopodia | cytoplasmic projection | movement + feeding | Amoeba |
| Flagella | whip-like organelle | propulsion | Euglena |
| Cilia | short numerous organelles | movement + food current | Paramecium |

## Adaptive Significance

Nutrition and locomotion are connected. Locomotion helps Protozoa reach food, avoid harmful stimuli, find suitable light, or move inside host/environment. Nutrition provides energy for movement and survival. This relationship shows that even a single cell can act as an integrated organism.
