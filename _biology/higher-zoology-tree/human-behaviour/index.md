Human Behaviour Node connects biology with daily decision-making. It treats behaviour as an output of nervous regulation, endocrine signalling, learning history, social environment, and reflective correction.

## Core Axes

- **Stimulus:** the external or internal trigger that begins a response.
- **Biological activation:** nervous, hormonal, muscular, and autonomic reaction.
- **CNS appraisal:** interpretation, attention, memory, emotion, and executive control.
- **Behavioural output:** habit, avoidance, aggression, cooperation, curiosity, or reflection.
- **Correction loop:** feedback, learning, self-regulation, and ethical decision.

## Learning Pathway

1. Observe the stimulus without immediate judgement.
2. Identify the biological response: heartbeat, breathing, posture, attention, or fatigue.
3. Ask which CNS pathway is leading the action: reflex, habit, fear, reward, reasoning, or service.
4. Reframe the behaviour using evidence, empathy, and reflective discipline.
