# Study of Permanent Slides

NU syllabus অনুযায়ী **at least 20 slides** study করতে হবে। নিচে **43-preparation reference bank** দেওয়া হলো; departmental availability ও course requirement অনুযায়ী এখান থেকে ≥20 preparation নির্বাচন করা যাবে।

## A. Whole Animals — Protozoans, Rotifers & Arthropods

| Slide | What to identify | Diagnostic cues |
|---|---|---|
| *Amoeba proteus* | whole mount | irregular outline, pseudopodia, nucleus, food vacuoles |
| *Paramecium caudatum* | whole mount | slipper-shaped body, cilia, oral groove, macro/micronucleus |
| *Euglena viridis* | whole mount | spindle body, flagellum, chloroplasts, eyespot |
| *Vorticella* | whole mount | bell-shaped zooid, contractile stalk, peristomial cilia |
| Rotifer (*Brachionus*) | whole mount | corona, lorica, mastax, posterior foot |
| *Daphnia* | whole mount | bivalved carapace, large second antennae, compound eye, brood chamber |
| *Cyclops* | whole mount | unpaired median eye, long antennules, narrow abdomen, paired egg sacs in female |
| *Moina* | whole mount | cladoceran body, large antennae, brood chamber |

> **Modern terminology note:** syllabus-এর “protozoans” traditional teaching term; modern taxonomy-তে Amoeba, Paramecium ও Euglena Animalia-এর অংশ নয়।

## B. Mouth Parts of Arthropods

| Slide | Type | Key structures |
|---|---|---|
| Cockroach | biting & chewing | labrum, mandibles, maxillae, labium, hypopharynx |
| Grasshopper | biting & chewing | strong mandibles + maxillae/labium |
| Mosquito | piercing & sucking | elongated proboscis; stylets within labial sheath |
| Housefly | sponging | labium ending in labella with pseudotracheae |
| Butterfly/moth | siphoning | coiled proboscis formed chiefly by maxillary galeae |
| Honeybee | chewing-lapping | mandibles + elongated labium/maxillae |

### Slide-answer rule
Mouthpart slide-এ “type” বলাই যথেষ্ট নয়; **which parts are modified and what function they perform**—এটিও লিখবে।

## E. Histological Slides of Invertebrates

Suggested set:

1. Earthworm T.S. — cuticle, epidermis, circular/longitudinal muscle, coelom, gut, typhlosole, dorsal/ventral vessels.
2. Leech T.S. — reduced coelom, muscular body wall, gut/diverticula.
3. Cockroach intestine T.S. — epithelium, lumen, muscle layers.
4. Prawn gill section — gill lamellae/filaments and vascular spaces.
5. Molluscan gill section — lamellae and ciliated epithelium.
6. Testis/ovary of an invertebrate — germ-cell sequence.
7. Fasciola T.S. — tegument, gut, reproductive tissue.
8. Nematode T.S. — cuticle, hypodermis, longitudinal muscles, pseudocoel, gut/reproductive tube.

## Microscope Workflow

1. Slide label না দেখে প্রথমে low power-এ overall pattern observe করো।
2. Specimen center করো।
3. Low → high power progression অনুসরণ করো।
4. Diagnostic structure identify করার পরে label check করো।
5. Drawing-এ field border, scale/magnification এবং essential labels রাখো।
6. Histology-তে artefact ও true tissue boundary আলাদা করো।
