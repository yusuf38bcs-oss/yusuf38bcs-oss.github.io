# Temporary Mounting

## General Rule

Temporary mount-এ structure intact, moist এবং minimally distorted রাখতে হবে। Coverslip pressure সবচেয়ে common source of error।

---

# 1. Brain of Earthworm

## Aim
Cerebral ganglia এবং circumpharyngeal connectives demonstrate করা।

## Procedure logic
1. Anterior few segments isolate/secure করো।
2. Dorsal body wall open করে pharyngeal region expose।
3. Pharynx-এর dorsal side-এ paired **cerebral ganglia** locate।
4. Fine needle দিয়ে surrounding connective tissue remove।
5. Circumpharyngeal connectives যতটা সম্ভব preserve করে brain isolate।
6. Saline/glycerin-based temporary medium-এ transfer।
7. Coverslip gently lower; crush করবে না।

## Identify
- paired cerebral ganglia;
- commissural connection;
- circumpharyngeal connectives.

## Identification
> Paired supra-pharyngeal cerebral ganglia ও circumpharyngeal connectives থাকার কারণে preparation-টি earthworm brain।

---

# 2. Salivary Gland of Cockroach

## Aim
Paired salivary glands, reservoirs and ducts demonstrate করা।

## Procedure logic
1. Specimen ventral/dorsal access departmental method অনুযায়ী open করো।
2. Crop region-এর পাশে whitish lobulated salivary tissue identify।
3. Paired glands এবং transparent reservoirs trace।
4. Fine forceps/needles দিয়ে gland-reservoir unit detach।
5. Saline-এ wash।
6. Slide-এ spread করে coverslip gently apply।

## Identify
- glandular lobules/acini;
- salivary reservoirs;
- ducts.

## Common error
Fat body-কে salivary gland ধরে নেওয়া। Salivary gland more organized lobulated network; reservoir thin-walled sac।

---

# 3. Statocyst of Prawn

## Aim
Antennular statocyst and statolith demonstrate করা।

## Location
Statocyst usually antennule-এর basal segment (**precoxa / basal podomere region**) associated cavity।

## Procedure logic
1. Antennule detach করো।
2. Basal segment isolate।
3. Fine needles দিয়ে wall carefully open।
4. Statocyst cavity expose।
5. Sensory setae এবং sand-grain/statolith material disturb না করে mount।
6. Low power-এ cavity and internal material observe।

## Identify
- statocyst wall;
- sensory setae;
- statolith/sand particles where retained.

## Functional interpretation
Statocyst equilibrium/orientation sense-এ সাহায্য করে। Molting-এর পরে many prawns external particles ব্যবহার করে statolith পুনর্গঠন করতে পারে।

# 7-mark Temporary Mount Checklist

A high-quality preparation should show:

- correct specimen/organ selected;
- minimal tearing;
- correct orientation;
- mount not too thick;
- no major air bubble over target;
- diagnostic structures visible;
- drawing properly labelled.
