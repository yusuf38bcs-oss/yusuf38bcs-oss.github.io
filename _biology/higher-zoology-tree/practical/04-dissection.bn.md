# External Morphology and Dissection

## Syllabus Map

### Major dissection
- Circulatory system — **earthworm, prawn**
- Nervous system — **cockroach, grasshopper, prawn, Pila, Lamellidens**
- Reproductive system — **earthworm, cockroach, grasshopper, prawn**

### Minor dissection
- Digestive system — **prawn, Pila, Lamellidens**
- Nervous system — **cockroach, grasshopper, prawn**

> Major/minor overlap syllabus অনুযায়ী preserve করা হয়েছে; examination instruction অনুযায়ী display depth আলাদা হতে পারে।

## General Dissection Rules

1. Specimen আগে external orientation নিশ্চিত করো।
2. Pinning এমনভাবে করবে যাতে target organ-system stretch বা tear না হয়।
3. Cut line shallow রাখবে; internal organs blind-cut করবে না।
4. Dissection needle দিয়ে connective tissue separate করবে—organ টেনে ছিঁড়বে না।
5. Display-তে unnecessary tissue remove না করে target structure expose করবে।
6. Wet specimen শুকাতে দেবে না।
7. Drawing-এর orientation dissection tray-এর orientation-এর সঙ্গে consistent হবে।

# A. External Morphology

## Earthworm
Label: prostomium, mouth, segments, clitellum, dorsal pores, setae, genital pores, anus.

## Cockroach
Label: head, antennae, compound eyes, pronotum, fore/hind wings, three leg pairs, abdominal segments, cerci; male/female terminal differences.

## Prawn
Label: rostrum, carapace, stalked eyes, antennules, antennae, pereiopods, abdominal segments, pleopods, telson, uropods.

## *Pila*
Label: shell apex, spire, sutures, body whorl, aperture, operculum; living specimen-এ head, tentacles, foot.

## *Lamellidens*
Label: valves, umbo, hinge line, growth lines; opened specimen-এ mantle, gills, foot, siphonal region.

# B. Major Dissections

## 1. Earthworm — Circulatory System

### Aim
Dorsal vessel, ventral vessel, hearts/aortic arches এবং প্রধান vascular connections expose করা।

### Orientation
Specimen dorsal side upward; anterior end identify করো।

### Display sequence
1. Shallow mid-dorsal incision।
2. Body wall lateral pinning।
3. Septa carefully cut where needed।
4. Alimentary canal-এর dorsal surface বরাবর **dorsal blood vessel** trace।
5. Anterior region-এ paired **hearts/aortic arches** identify।
6. Gut gently displace করে ventral vessel দেখানো।
7. Nephridia/reproductive organs unnecessarily damage করবে না।

### Final labels
Dorsal vessel, ventral vessel, lateral hearts/aortic arches, intestinal vessels.

### Common error
Dorsal vessel-কে gut wall-এর pigmented line ধরে নেওয়া; vessel continuity verify করবে।

---

## 2. Prawn — Circulatory System

### Aim
Dorsal heart and major arterial origins demonstrate করা।

### Display sequence
1. Dorsal carapace carefully remove।
2. Pericardial region expose।
3. Dorsal polygonal/triangular **heart** identify।
4. Ostia observe where possible।
5. Anterior and posterior arterial branches trace without tearing hepatopancreas/gonad unnecessarily।

### Labels
Heart, pericardial sinus, ophthalmic/anterior arteries, posterior abdominal artery, sternal artery where demonstrable.

---

## 3. Cockroach — Nervous System

### Aim
Brain, circumoesophageal connectives, suboesophageal ganglion এবং double ventral nerve cord expose করা।

### Display sequence
1. Dorsal dissection দিয়ে alimentary canal gently remove/displace।
2. Head region-এ cerebral ganglia identify।
3. Circumoesophageal connectives trace।
4. Suboesophageal ganglion থেকে paired ventral nerve cords follow।
5. Thoracic and abdominal ganglia serially identify।

### Labels
Brain, circumoesophageal connectives, suboesophageal ganglion, thoracic ganglia, abdominal ganglia, ventral nerve cord.

---

## 4. Grasshopper — Nervous System

Cockroach-এর একই general logic, তবে body proportions আলাদা।  
Display:
- brain;
- suboesophageal ganglion;
- thoracic ganglia;
- abdominal ganglia;
- paired longitudinal connectives.

---

## 5. Prawn — Nervous System

### Main structures
Supraoesophageal ganglion (brain), circumoesophageal connectives, suboesophageal ganglion, thoracic ganglionic mass, abdominal nerve cord/ganglia.

### Technique principle
Digestive tract এবং ventral tissues carefully displace করে nerve chain expose করবে; abdomen-এ ventral midline follow করা সহজ।

---

## 6. *Pila* — Nervous System

### Diagnostic feature
Gastropod torsion-এর context-এ ganglionic nervous system; cerebral, pleural, pedal, buccal এবং visceral ganglia/connectives।

### Display targets
- cerebral ganglia;
- buccal ganglia;
- pedal ganglia;
- pleural ganglia;
- visceral loop/connectives.

### Common error
Connective vs commissure গুলিয়ে ফেলা: same-side ganglia linking = connective; paired opposite ganglia linking = commissure.

---

## 7. *Lamellidens* — Nervous System

### Display targets
- cerebral/ cerebropleural ganglia near mouth;
- pedal ganglia in foot;
- visceral ganglia posteriorly;
- longitudinal connectives.

Bivalve nervous system dispersed; gastropod-এর মতো compact brain expect করবে না।

---

## 8. Earthworm — Reproductive System

### Main structures
Testes/testis sacs (small, anterior), seminal vesicles, spermathecae, ovaries, oviducts, male ducts, prostate glands (species-specific practical), genital pores.

### Display logic
Anterior reproductive segments carefully expose; gut সম্পূর্ণ remove না করে structures side-by-side demonstrate করবে।

---

## 9. Cockroach — Reproductive System

### Male
Paired testes, vasa deferentia, seminal vesicles, ejaculatory duct, mushroom/accessory glands, genital pouch/phallomeres.

### Female
Paired ovaries (ovarioles), lateral oviducts, common oviduct/vagina, spermatheca, collateral/accessory glands.

### Key comparison
Male: slender ducts + accessory glands.  
Female: conspicuous ovaries with multiple ovarioles.

---

## 10. Grasshopper — Reproductive System

### Male
Testes, vasa deferentia, seminal vesicles/accessory glands, ejaculatory duct.

### Female
Ovaries with ovarioles, lateral oviducts, common oviduct, spermatheca and accessory glands.

---

## 11. Prawn — Reproductive System

### Male
Paired testes, vasa deferentia, terminal male genital openings (species/group dependent).

### Female
Paired/connected ovary dorsal to hepatopancreatic region, oviducts, female genital openings.

### Sex clue
Pleopod modifications and genital pore positions can support sex identification.

---

# C. Minor Dissections

## 12. Prawn — Digestive System
Label: mouth, oesophagus, cardiac stomach, pyloric stomach, hepatopancreas, midgut/intestine, rectum, anus.

## 13. *Pila* — Digestive System
Label: mouth/buccal mass, oesophagus, stomach, digestive gland, intestine, rectum, anus. Radula belongs to buccal apparatus and may be separately demonstrated.

## 14. *Lamellidens* — Digestive System
Label: mouth, oesophagus, stomach, digestive diverticula, intestine, rectum, anus. Note: bivalves lack a radula.

## 15–17. Minor Nervous-system Displays
Cockroach, grasshopper and prawn-এর জন্য major-dissection page-এর same anatomy ব্যবহার করবে, কিন্তু minor display-এ **clean exposure + principal labels**-এ focus থাকবে; unnecessary fine branches chase করবে না।

# Dissection Drawing Template

Every drawing:
- title;
- dorsal/ventral orientation;
- anterior/posterior direction;
- 6–12 essential labels;
- clean single lines;
- no artistic shading;
- scale not mandatory unless department asks.
