# Field Visit, Collection & Scientific Report

## Syllabus Requirement

- local invertebrate fauna observe **or** farm visit;
- at least **10 samples** collect;
- preserve and label;
- laboratory-তে submit;
- scientific report prepare;
- natural/hypothetical community-তে **quadrat method** দিয়ে population density;
- same community-র **Shannon–Wiener diversity index** calculate।

## Ethical Collection Rule

Collection learning-এর জন্য minimum necessary specimen নেবে। Protected, threatened বা legally restricted organism collect করবে না। Farm/private land-এ permission নিতে হবে। Habitat damage এবং non-target mortality minimize করবে।

```text

```

Unknown sample-এ false species name দেবে না; “sp. / morphotype” লিখে later verify করবে।

## Field Notebook

Record:
- site description;
- weather;
- habitat type;
- substrate/vegetation;
- sampling effort;
- microhabitat;
- behaviour when alive;
- photo number if photographed;
- sample ID cross-reference.

## Quadrat Method

### Population Density

If equal-area quadrats:

\[
\text{Density}=
\frac{\text{Total individuals of species}}
{\text{Total quadrat area sampled}}
\]

For \(q\) quadrats each of area \(a\):

\[
\text{Total area}=q\times a
\]

### Mean count per quadrat

\[
\bar{x}=\frac{\sum x_i}{q}
\]

### Frequency

\[
\text{Frequency (\%)}=
\frac{\text{No. of quadrats where species occurs}}
{\text{Total quadrats}}
\times 100
\]

Density এবং frequency একই ecological metric নয়।

## Community Diversity

For each taxon:

\[
p_i=\frac{n_i}{N}
\]

Shannon–Wiener:

\[
H'=-\sum p_i\ln p_i
\]

Always state:
- logarithm base used (natural log recommended);
- taxonomic resolution;
- sample size;
- whether data are pooled across quadrats.

## Scientific Report Structure

### Title
Specific, site + organism/community + method clear।

### Abstract
Question, method, key result এবং conclusion এক short paragraph-এ।

### Introduction
- ecological context;
- why invertebrate sampling matters;
- objective/research question;
- no invented literature claim.

### Materials and Methods
- site;
- dates;
- quadrat size and number;
- sample collection;
- identification method;
- preservation;
- formulas;
- ethical/permission note.

### Results
Only observed/calculated data:
- sample inventory;
- density;
- frequency;
- Shannon;
- tables/figures.

### Discussion
Interpret patterns:
- dominance;
- habitat association;
- sampling bias;
- comparison among taxa/sites;
- limitations.

Do not convert correlation into causation without experiment/evidence।

### Acknowledgement
Field/lab assistance, institution/permission source।

### References
Books, taxonomic databases and any scientific papers actually used।

## Sample Submission Checklist

- at least 10;
- unique Sample ID;
- label readable and preservative-resistant;
- container sealed;
- collection locality/date present;
- preliminary identification honest;
- specimen condition acceptable;
- field notebook cross-reference present.

## Socratic Review

Before finalizing the report ask:

1. আমার conclusion কি actual data-এর চেয়ে বেশি claim করছে?
2. Sampling effort unequal ছিল কি?
3. Identification certainty species-level claim justify করে কি?
4. Quadrat method mobile animals-এর জন্য কী limitation তৈরি করেছে?
5. Shannon value difference ecological difference, sampling difference, নাকি দুটোই হতে পারে?
