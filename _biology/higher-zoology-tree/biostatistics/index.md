## Study Strategy

1. Identify the biological question and variable type.
2. Summarize the data before testing a hypothesis.
3. State assumptions and the analysis method explicitly.
4. Recalculate worked examples rather than memorizing a final value.
5. Report effect estimates and uncertainty, not significance labels alone.
6. Separate statistical association from causal inference.

## Course Boundary

This material is educational. Statistical procedures should be matched to the actual design, sampling process and data-generating assumptions of a real study.
