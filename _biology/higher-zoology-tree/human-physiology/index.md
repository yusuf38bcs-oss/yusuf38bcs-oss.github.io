Human Physiology is organized here as one **28-lecture canonical course** rather than a collection of competing digestive, respiratory and circulatory mini-courses.

## Start Here

[Open the complete 28-lecture course index]({{ '/biology/higher-zoology-tree/human-physiology/course-index/' | relative_url }})

## Course Architecture

- Lectures 01–14: digestive system foundations and revision
- Lectures 15–18: respiratory physiology and revision
- Lecture 19: baroreceptor reflex bridge
- Lectures 20–25: blood and cardiovascular physiology
- Lectures 26–28: honours-level digestive physiology and metabolic interpretation

## Study Strategy

Observe the physiological problem, identify the regulated variable, follow the mechanism, connect structure with function, then explain the biological consequence. This course-level strategy replaces repeated generic learning-framework panels on every lecture page.

## Educational Boundary

Health and disease examples are provided for physiology education. They do not replace individual medical diagnosis, treatment or emergency advice.
