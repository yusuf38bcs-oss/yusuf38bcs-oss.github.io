# Genetics Lecture 07: Epistasis and Gene Ratios

Epistasis occurs when one gene influences the visible effect of another gene. It explains modified inheritance ratios.

## Learning Focus

- dominant epistasis
- recessive epistasis
- complementary pattern
- duplicate pattern

## Practice

1. Define epistasis.
2. Explain why ratios may change.
