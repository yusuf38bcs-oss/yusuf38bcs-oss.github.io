Genetics explains how biological information is stored, expressed, transmitted, and varied across generations. This gateway links Mendelian inheritance, chromosome behaviour, molecular genetics, mutation, and applied problem-solving.

## Core Learning Route

- **Classical genetics:** Mendel's laws, monohybrid cross, dihybrid cross, test cross, and probability.
- **Chromosomal basis:** gene linkage, crossing over, sex determination, and chromosomal variation.
- **Molecular basis:** DNA, RNA, replication, transcription, translation, codon logic, and gene expression.
- **Applied genetics:** mutation, pedigree analysis, biotechnology, and genetic reasoning.

## Study Sequence

1. Master the vocabulary: gene, allele, genotype, phenotype, homozygous, heterozygous, dominant, recessive.
2. Draw Punnett squares before solving numerical problems.
3. Connect chromosome behaviour with inheritance ratios.
4. Move from Mendelian ratios to molecular explanation.
5. Finish with MCQ and short-answer practice.
