# Genetics Lecture 03: Mendel and Pea Plant

Gregor Johann Mendel studied inheritance using pea plant. Pea plant was useful because it is annual, self-pollinating, easy to cross, and has clear contrasting traits.

## Learning Focus

- Who Mendel was
- Why pea plant was selected
- Meaning of pure line and hybrid
- Scientific value of controlled crossing
