# Digestive System Lecture 14

এই lecture পুরো Digestive System chapter-এর revision map।

## Master Flow

Food intake → chewing → saliva mixing → swallowing → stomach mixing → intestinal digestion → nutrient uptake → water recovery

## Organ Map

- Mouth: food preparation.
- Stomach: storage and mixing.
- Liver: bile production.
- Pancreas: digestive juice support.
- Small intestine: final digestion and nutrient uptake.
- Large intestine: water recovery.
