# Digestive System Lecture 14: Master Revision and Exam Map

## Purpose of This Revision

এই lecture পুরো Digestive System chapter-এর final revision map। এখানে structure, secretion, movement, digestion pathway, absorption, regulation এবং health issue একসঙ্গে সাজানো হয়েছে।

## Master Flow

```text
Food intake
  ↓
Mastication and saliva mixing
  ↓
Bolus formation and swallowing
  ↓
Oesophageal transport
  ↓
Stomach storage, mixing and chyme formation
  ↓
Bile and pancreatic juice in duodenum
  ↓
Carbohydrate, amino acid and lipid pathways
  ↓
Absorption through villi and microvilli
  ↓
Water recovery in large intestine
  ↓
Defecation
```

## Organ Map

| Organ | Main role | Must-remember point |
|---|---|---|
| Mouth | food preparation | chewing, saliva, bolus |
| Oesophagus | transport | peristalsis |
| Stomach | storage and mixing | chyme and early protein work |
| Liver | bile production | bile is not enzyme |
| Gallbladder | bile storage | releases bile to duodenum |
| Pancreas | digestive juice | mixed gland |
| Small intestine | final digestion and absorption | villi and microvilli |
| Large intestine | water recovery | faeces formation |

## Enzyme and Secretion Map

| Source | Secretion | Main target |
|---|---|---|
| Salivary gland | saliva | starch digestion starts |
| Gastric gland | gastric juice | stomach phase |
| Liver | bile | lipid emulsification |
| Pancreas | pancreatic juice | carbohydrate, protein, lipid digestion support |
| Intestinal gland | intestinal juice | final digestion |

## Nutrient Pathway Summary

### Carbohydrate

```text
Starch → maltose-type sugar → glucose/fructose/galactose → blood
```

### Amino Acid Pathway

```text
Dietary protein → peptide fragments → amino acids → blood
```

### Lipid

```text
Fat → emulsified droplets → fatty acid and glycerol pathway → lymph/blood support
```

## Frequently Confused Ideas

| Confusion | Correct idea |
|---|---|
| Digestion = absorption | Digestion breaks food; absorption carries nutrient into body fluids |
| Bile is enzyme | Bile is not enzyme; it emulsifies fat |
| Stomach completes digestion | Stomach begins and mixes; intestine completes most digestion |
| Villi = microvilli | Villi are larger projections; microvilli are cell-surface projections |
| Rectum = anus | Rectum stores; anus is final opening |

## Final Summary

Digestive system একটি integrated biological system। কোনো organ একা কাজ করে না। Mouth preparation করে, oesophagus transport করে, stomach chyme তৈরি করে, liver bile দেয়, pancreas digestive juice দেয়, small intestine final digestion ও absorption করে, আর large intestine water recovery ও waste removal সম্পন্ন করে।
