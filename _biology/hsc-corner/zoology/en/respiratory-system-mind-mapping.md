# Respiratory System Revision Map

This English mirror gives a compact revision map of the respiratory system series. Use it after reading the full lectures to check sequence, structure, function and exam readiness.

## Master Map

```text
Respiratory System
  ├─ Air route
  │   └─ nose → pharynx → larynx → trachea → bronchi → bronchioles → alveoli
  ├─ Conducting region
  │   └─ carries and prepares air
  ├─ Exchange region
  │   └─ alveolar surface and capillary network
  ├─ Breathing mechanics
  │   └─ volume and pressure change
  ├─ Transport logic
  │   └─ oxygen and carbon dioxide movement
  └─ Awareness
      └─ clean air, protection, and responsible behaviour
```

## Revision Questions

1. Why is the alveolus called the functional unit?
2. How does diaphragm movement change air flow?
3. Why are branching bronchioles useful?
4. How does a thin surface support efficient exchange?
5. Which part of the pathway protects the lower airway?

## Final Insight

The respiratory system is best learned as a route plus a mechanism. First learn the pathway, then learn why each step is necessary.
