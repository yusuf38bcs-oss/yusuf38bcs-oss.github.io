# Human Respiratory System Part 3: Respiratory Awareness and Public Biology

This English mirror keeps the lecture within an academic biology frame. The focus is the relationship between airway structure, lung function, environment, protection, and community-level awareness.

## Biological Reasoning Map

```text
Air quality and exposure
  ↓
Airway response
  ↓
Ventilation efficiency
  ↓
Alveolar exchange condition
  ↓
Whole-body oxygen supply logic
```

## Learning Focus

Students should connect respiratory anatomy with real-life biological reasoning. Clean air, ventilation, protective behaviour, and awareness all become easier to understand when the learner knows how the nasal passage, trachea, bronchi, bronchioles, and alveoli work together.

## Learner Outcomes

After this lesson, learners should be able to identify which part of the respiratory system is involved in a given scenario, explain how environment affects breathing efficiency, and write a concise biology-based awareness answer for examination use.
