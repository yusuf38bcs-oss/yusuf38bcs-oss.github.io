This Botany hub organizes plant-life learning from the cellular level to genetic information flow. It is designed for HSC learners, admission preparation, and reflective biology practice.

## Core Learning Route

- **Cell architecture:** nucleus, ribosome, mitochondria, chloroplast, endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi body, lysosome, vacuole, and cytoskeleton.
- **Genetic information:** DNA, RNA, chromosome, gene expression, and central dogma.
- **Plant structure:** tissues, organs, transport, photosynthesis, growth, and reproduction.
- **Applied reasoning:** connect structure with function and function with survival.

## How to Study This Hub

1. Read the organelle notes first.
2. Draw a cell map from memory.
3. Link each organelle to one biological function.
4. Move from cell structure to gene expression and inheritance.
5. Finish with short-answer and MCQ practice.
