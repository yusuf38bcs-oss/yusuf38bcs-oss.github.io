<h2>Key Terms</h2><div class="grid"><div class="term"><strong>Golgi apparatus</strong><span>Stacked cisternae-যুক্ত modification, sorting ও packaging organelle.</span></div><div class="term"><strong>Cisternae</strong><span>Golgi-এর flattened membrane sacs.</span></div><div class="term"><strong>Cis face</strong><span>ER-facing receiving side.</span></div><div class="term"><strong>Trans face</strong><span>Shipping/sorting side.</span></div><div class="term"><strong>Lysosome</strong><span>Acidic hydrolase-containing intracellular digestion compartment.</span></div><div class="term"><strong>Autophagy</strong><span>Damaged cellular component lysosomal degradation ও recycling.</span></div><div class="term"><strong>Peroxisome</strong><span>Single-membrane oxidative organelle.</span></div><div class="term"><strong>Catalase</strong><span>Hydrogen peroxide-কে water ও oxygen-এ breakdown করা enzyme.</span></div></div>

<h2>Core Concept Explanation</h2>
<h3>Golgi Body: Receive → Modify → Sort → Ship</h3>
<p>Golgi apparatus stacked flattened <strong>cisternae</strong> দিয়ে গঠিত। <strong>Cis face</strong> ER থেকে transport vesicle receive করে; cisternae-এর মধ্য দিয়ে cargo progress করার সময় carbohydrate বা other group modification, quality processing ও sorting ঘটে; <strong>trans-Golgi network</strong> destination অনুযায়ী vesicle package করে। Plant cell-এ individual Golgi stack-কে প্রায়ই <strong>dictyosome</strong> বলা হয়।</p>
<p>Golgi protein synthesize করে না। Ribosome synthesis করে, Rough ER initial folding/processing pathway দেয়, Golgi further modification ও sorting করে। Golgi অনেক animal cell-এ lysosomal enzyme targeting ও lysosome biogenesis pathway-এ গুরুত্বপূর্ণ।</p>

<h3>Lysosome: Controlled Digestion and Recycling</h3>
<p>Lysosome single-membrane acidic compartment; এতে acid hydrolase থাকে। Endocytosed material, macromolecule ও damaged organelle breakdown করে। <strong>Autophagy</strong>-তে damaged cellular component lysosome-এর সঙ্গে fusion করে degrade ও recycle হয়। “Suicide bag” label alone misleading—normal cell-এ lysosome highly regulated maintenance organelle।</p>
<p>Plant cell-এ classic lysosome-এর অনেক lytic function central vacuole বা lytic vacuole পালন করতে পারে। তাই animal-cell description plant cell-এ mechanically copy করা ঠিক নয়।</p>

<h3>Peroxisome: Oxidative Protection</h3>
<p>Peroxisome single-membrane organelle; oxidase enzyme fatty acid ও other substrate oxidize করতে পারে এবং এই reaction-এ hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂) তৈরি হতে পারে। <strong>Catalase</strong> H₂O₂-কে water ও oxygen-এ convert করে toxicity কমায়। Peroxisome oxidative metabolism ও detoxification-এ কাজ করে, কিন্তু mitochondria-এর মতো main ATP-producing organelle নয়।</p>
<p>Plant peroxisome photorespiration-এ অংশ নিতে পারে; seed-এর specialized peroxisome <strong>glyoxysome</strong> stored lipid mobilization-এ গুরুত্বপূর্ণ—এগুলো extension concept।</p>

<section class="lbfl-flow"><h2>DOT — Core Logic</h2><div class="chain"><span>Ribosome → Rough ER → transport vesicle</span><span>Cis Golgi → modification → trans Golgi</span><span>Lysosome → digestion/recycling | Peroxisome → oxidative protection</span></div></section>

<section class="lbfl-flow"><h2>LINE — Application and Connection</h2><p>Protein secretion একটি supply chain: mRNA instruction → ribosome synthesis → Rough ER folding → vesicle transport → Golgi modification/sorting → secretory or membrane destination। Lysosome cleanup এবং peroxisome chemical protection এই delivery system-এর সঙ্গে cell maintenance সম্পূর্ণ করে।</p></section>

<section class="lbfl-thinking"><h2>CIRCLE — Reflection and System Thinking</h2><p>একটি sustainable system useful product organize করে, damaged part recycle করে এবং harmful by-product neutralize করে। Golgi, lysosome ও peroxisome এই তিনটি management principle-এর cellular example।</p></section>

<section><h2>Misconception Correction</h2><div class="mistake"><b>ভুল ধারণা:</b> Golgi body protein তৈরি করে।<br><strong>সংশোধন:</strong> Protein ribosome synthesize করে; Golgi modify, sort ও package করে।</div><div class="mistake"><b>ভুল ধারণা:</b> Cis ও trans face একই side।<br><strong>সংশোধন:</strong> Cis face receiving side; trans face sorting/shipping side।</div><div class="mistake"><b>ভুল ধারণা:</b> Lysosome শুধু cell destroy করে।<br><strong>সংশোধন:</strong> Normal lysosome controlled digestion, defense, autophagy ও recycling চালায়।</div><div class="mistake"><b>ভুল ধারণা:</b> Plant cell-এর lysosome animal cell-এর মতো সবসময় distinct।<br><strong>সংশোধন:</strong> Plant lytic vacuole lysosome-like function করতে পারে।</div><div class="mistake"><b>ভুল ধারণা:</b> Lysosome ও peroxisome একই।<br><strong>সংশোধন:</strong> Lysosome hydrolase ও acidic digestion; peroxisome oxidase/catalase ও oxidative metabolism।</div><div class="mistake"><b>ভুল ধারণা:</b> Peroxisome H₂O₂ শুধু তৈরি করে।<br><strong>সংশোধন:</strong> Oxidase H₂O₂ generate করতে পারে; catalase তা breakdown করে।</div><div class="mistake"><b>ভুল ধারণা:</b> Peroxisome ATP powerhouse।<br><strong>সংশোধন:</strong> Main ATP production mitochondria-এর সঙ্গে যুক্ত।</div></section>
