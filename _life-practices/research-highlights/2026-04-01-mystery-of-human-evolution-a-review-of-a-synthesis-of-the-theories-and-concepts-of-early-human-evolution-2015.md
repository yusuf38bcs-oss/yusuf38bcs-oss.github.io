          </ul>

        <summary>II. Environmental Drivers of Adaptation</summary>
        <div class="node-content">

          <ul style="padding-left: 1.25rem; color: #94a3b8; margin-top: 0.5rem;">
            <li><strong>The Savannah Hypothesis:</strong> Analyzing how the open grasslands of the East African Rift System impacted the evolution of upright locomotion.</li>
            <li><strong>Variability Selection Paradigm:</strong> Evaluating how fluctuating climate cycles favored flexible, adaptive behavioral strategies over hyper-specialized physical traits.</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </details>

        <summary>III. Lithic Tech-Nodes & Behavioral Archeology</summary>

            <li><strong>Oldowan Core Industries:</strong> Early stone tools indicating basic percussion fracturing techniques.</li>
            <li><strong>Acheulean Bifacial Advancements:</strong> Symmetrical handaxes that showcase mental templates and deliberate design planning.</li>
          </ul>
