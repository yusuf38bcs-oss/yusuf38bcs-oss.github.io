Band 8 requires accurate understanding, clear organization, flexible vocabulary, grammatical control, and the ability to develop ideas naturally rather than reproduce memorised templates.

## Learning pathway

1. Diagnose current strengths and weaknesses.
2. Complete one focused 20-minute session daily.
3. Practise the weakest question type separately.
4. Compare the attempt with a model answer or criterion checklist.
5. Record recurring errors and rewrite or repeat the weak section.
6. Complete a weekly mini mock and review progress.

## Four-skill rotation

**Listening → Reading → Writing → Speaking**
