# IELTS Writing Lab

Practise Task 1 and Task 2 through planning, timed writing, self-assessment, model-answer comparison, and focused rewriting.

## Band criteria

- Task Achievement / Task Response
- Coherence and Cohesion
- Lexical Resource
- Grammatical Range and Accuracy

> Word counter, timer, prompt bank, and error tagging are scheduled for the interactive MVP.
