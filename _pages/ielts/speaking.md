Build fluency, idea development, lexical flexibility, grammatical control, and pronunciation awareness across Parts 1, 2, and 3.

## Foundation practice areas

- Part 1 warm-up questions
- Part 2 cue-card structure
- One-minute preparation strategy
- Part 3 opinion development
- Self-assessment and vocabulary upgrading

> Cue-card generation and preparation/speaking timers are scheduled for the interactive MVP.
