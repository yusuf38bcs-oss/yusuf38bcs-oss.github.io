Accuracy matters in Biology education. We welcome reports of factual errors, unclear wording, broken links, accessibility barriers, and missing or misleading references.

## How to report an issue

Use the [Contact]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}) page and include the page address, the relevant passage or feature, and any supporting source where available.

## What happens next

We assess reports against textbooks, authoritative institutional material, or other appropriate evidence. Substantive corrections are made as soon as reasonably practical. When a correction materially changes an explanation, assessment, or learner decision, we will note the update on the affected page where feasible.

## Editorial independence

Correction requests are evaluated on evidence. Commercial, political, or personal pressure does not determine scientific or educational claims.
