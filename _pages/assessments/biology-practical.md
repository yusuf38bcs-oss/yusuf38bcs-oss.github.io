## Purpose of This Section

<section class="lbfl-assessment-panel">
  <p class="lbfl-assessment-lead">A good practical answer should show:</p>
  <ul class="lbfl-practical-checklist">

    <li>correct biological terminology</li>
    <li>labelled diagram or flowchart</li>

## Practical Rubric

<section class="lbfl-rubric-card">
  <p class="lbfl-rubric-kicker">Assessment Tool</p>
  <h3>Global Practical Biology Rubric</h3>
  <div class="lbfl-rubric-scale">
    <div class="lbfl-rubric-row"><b>Observation</b><span>Identifies visible structure or process accurately.</span></div>
    <div class="lbfl-rubric-row"><b>Identification</b><span>Uses correct biological name and relevant classification.</span></div>
    <div class="lbfl-rubric-row"><b>Diagram</b><span>Draws clean, proportionate, labelled figure or flowchart.</span></div>
    <div class="lbfl-rubric-row"><b>Labelling</b><span>Places labels correctly.</span></div>
    <div class="lbfl-rubric-row"><b>Explanation</b><span>Connects structure with function using precise logic.</span></div>
    <div class="lbfl-rubric-row"><b>Interpretation</b><span>Explains evidence, comparison, result, or specimen cue.</span></div>
  </div>
</section>
