# Rapid Revision Hub

Rapid Revision is a focused review zone for learners who need to revise a chapter quickly before class tests, model tests, viva, admission preparation, or final examination practice. The structure emphasizes short concept recall, high-yield facts, and quick correction of misconceptions.

## Revision Method

Use this section after finishing a full lesson. First, recall the topic from memory. Then answer the short MCQ set. Finally, read the explanation and validity logic to identify which concepts require another reading.

## What to Revise Here

- Key definitions and biological terms.
- Structure-function relationships.
- Sequential processes such as digestion, respiration, circulation, inheritance, and ecological succession.
- Diagram labels and common exam traps.
- Short explanatory logic behind correct and incorrect answers.

## Best Practice

For each rapid revision set, write three weak points in a notebook. Revisit the main lecture page, correct the weak points, and then attempt the set again after 24 hours. This converts rapid revision into long-term retention.
