# HSC Biology Model Test

<section class="lbfl-assessment-hero">
  <p class="lbfl-assessment-kicker">CQ Studio · Bloom Rubric · Board Pattern</p>
  <p class="lbfl-assessment-lead">The <strong>HSC Biology Model Test</strong> route is designed for Botany and Zoology exam preparation through structured revision, concept-based MCQ practice, board-style creative questions, diagram reasoning, and reflective correction. A model test must not train guessing; it must show whether a learner can recall, explain, apply, analyze, justify, and communicate biological ideas under exam conditions.</p>

<section class="lbfl-assessment-panel">
  <p class="lbfl-rubric-kicker">Global Rubric</p>

  <div class="lbfl-rubric-scale">

    <div class="lbfl-rubric-row"><b>Apply</b><span>Use a concept in a stimulus, diagram, data set, specimen, or unfamiliar example.</span><span>Typical CQ: গ</span></div>

## CQ Studio: Board-Style Question Block

<section class="lbfl-cq-card">
  <p class="lbfl-cq-kicker">Creative Question Template</p>
  <h3>Board Pattern CQ Structure</h3>
  <div class="lbfl-cq-meta"><span class="lbfl-cq-tag">Marks: 10</span><span class="lbfl-cq-tag">Time: 18 minutes</span><span class="lbfl-cq-tag">Stimulus Based</span><span class="lbfl-cq-tag">Bloom: Remember → Evaluate</span></div>
  <div class="lbfl-cq-stimulus"><strong>Stimulus:</strong> A short biological situation, figure, diagram, table, experiment, specimen, or comparison cue. The stimulus must connect directly with the syllabus and should not require irrelevant outside knowledge.</div>
  <div class="lbfl-cq-parts">
    <div class="lbfl-cq-part"><span class="lbfl-cq-letter">ক</span><div><strong>Knowledge / Remember</strong><br>Define, identify, or name the core term.<small>Expected answer: concise and accurate.</small></div></div>
    <div class="lbfl-cq-part"><span class="lbfl-cq-letter">খ</span><div><strong>Understanding</strong><br>Explain why, how, or what the biological meaning is.<small>Expected answer: concept plus reason.</small></div></div>
    <div class="lbfl-cq-part"><span class="lbfl-cq-letter">গ</span><div><strong>Application</strong><br>Apply the concept to the stimulus, diagram, data, or organism.<small>Expected answer: stimulus-linked explanation.</small></div></div>
    <div class="lbfl-cq-part"><span class="lbfl-cq-letter">ঘ</span><div><strong>Analysis / Evaluation</strong><br>Compare, justify, analyze, or evaluate with biological evidence.<small>Expected answer: reasoned conclusion.</small></div></div>

## Recommended Model-Test Structure

<div class="lbfl-assessment-grid">
  <section class="lbfl-cq-card"><h3>MCQ</h3><p class="lbfl-cq-text">Terms, functions, diagrams, comparisons, and mechanism logic. Solve first, then read explanation.</p></section>
  <section class="lbfl-cq-card"><h3>Short Answer</h3><p class="lbfl-cq-text">Definitions, reasons, examples, and biological significance. Write concise answers.</p></section>
  <section class="lbfl-cq-card"><h3>Creative Question</h3><p class="lbfl-cq-text">Stem analysis, data or diagram interpretation, and application. Identify concept before writing.</p></section>
  <section class="lbfl-cq-card"><h3>Diagram</h3><p class="lbfl-cq-text">Labelled structure, flowchart, and process map. Draw with correct sequence.</p></section>
  <section class="lbfl-cq-card"><h3>Reflection</h3><p class="lbfl-cq-text">Mistake analysis and next revision target. Write a weakness list after scoring.</p></section>
</div>

## How to Use a Model Test Properly

<section class="lbfl-assessment-panel">
  <ol>
    <li><strong>Read the topic list:</strong> identify which chapters are included.</li>
    <li><strong>Attempt without help:</strong> use a timer and avoid notes.</li>
    <li><strong>Review wrong answers deeply:</strong> explain why the correct answer is valid.</li>
    <li><strong>Return to the lesson:</strong> revise concept, diagram, or wording weakness.</li>
    <li><strong>Repeat after correction:</strong> test similar concepts again.</li>
  </ol>
</section>
