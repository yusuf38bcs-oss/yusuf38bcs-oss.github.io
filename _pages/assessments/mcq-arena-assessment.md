# Assessment Hub

The Assessment section connects MCQ practice with constructive alignment. Every question should support a learning objective, test a specific concept, and guide the learner toward a clearer biological explanation. This page is for structured classroom assessment, self-evaluation, and Socratic reflection.

## What This Section Measures

Assessment here is not limited to memorization. It measures whether the learner can explain a mechanism, interpret a diagram, connect structure with function, and apply a biological principle to a new situation.

## Assessment Flow

1. Identify the topic and learning objective.
2. Attempt the MCQ or creative question without looking at the answer.
3. Compare your response with the correct answer.
4. Read the validity logic and explanation.
5. Write one sentence explaining why the wrong options are invalid.

## Learner Outcome

By using this section regularly, learners should become more accurate, more reflective, and more confident in biological reasoning. The goal is not only a higher score, but also a stronger thinking pathway.
