# Assessment Rubric

The Learning Biology For Life assessment rubric keeps MCQ, short answer, creative question, diagram, practical reasoning, and reflection aligned with learning outcomes.

This page is the canonical reference. Assessment pages should link here instead of repeating the full rubric.

## Rubric Levels

### Remember

The answer correctly recalls a term, structure, label, definition, or fact.

### Understand

The answer explains meaning, reason, function, or biological significance.

### Apply

The answer uses a concept in a stimulus, diagram, specimen, data set, practical context, or unfamiliar example.

### Analyze

The answer compares, classifies, separates parts, interprets relationships, or connects cause and effect.

### Evaluate

The answer justifies a conclusion using relevant biological evidence.

### Create

The answer constructs a diagram, flowchart, concept map, prediction, or structured explanation from learned concepts.

## Minimum Quality Standard

A Biology answer should be checked for:

- Correct terminology.
- Clear mechanism.
- Stimulus connection.
- Evidence-based reasoning.
- Logical sequence.
- Concise communication.

## Scoring Logic

A high-quality answer is not simply long. It must be accurate, relevant, and aligned with the question demand.

Use this order while reviewing:

1. Does the answer match the question?
2. Are the biological terms correct?
3. Is the mechanism explained?
4. Is the stimulus used where required?
5. Is the conclusion justified?
