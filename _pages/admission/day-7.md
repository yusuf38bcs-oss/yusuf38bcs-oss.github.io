  <section class="lbfl-assessment-panel">
    <h2>Today's loop</h2>
    <p><strong>Learn → recall without notes → solve → submit or check → explain → repair → retest.</strong> Use a notebook for working. The page is a guide, not a substitute for a full syllabus or an official question paper.</p>
  </section>

  <h2>Four subject capsules</h2>
  <div class="lbfl-info-grid">
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Physics</h3><ul><li>Attempt mechanics questions under test conditions. Do not open notes between questions.</li><li>After scoring, classify each miss: principle, diagram, equation, algebra, unit, sign or time management.</li><li>Retest one representative question after repair, not only the exact remembered item.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Chemistry</h3><ul><li>Attempt mole, atomic and bonding questions in one uninterrupted block.</li><li>For each miss, write the smallest rule that would prevent recurrence.</li><li>Separate a knowledge gap from a calculation slip before planning revision.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Higher Mathematics</h3><ul><li>Attempt limits, differentiation, tangent and maxima–minima questions under time.</li><li>Mark where the method changed: limit, derivative, stationary condition or classification.</li><li>Redo one problem from a blank page after correction.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Biology</h3><ul><li>Attempt cell and Genetics questions from memory.</li><li>Use the existing Biology source routes for repair rather than relying on answer memorisation.</li><li>Record whether the error was vocabulary, sequence, mechanism or distractor discrimination.</li></ul></section>
  </div>

  <h2>Recall without notes</h2>
  <ol><li>What is the difference between knowing an answer and being able to justify it?</li><li>Which error type appeared more than once this week?</li><li>Which source page will you revisit before the retest?</li><li>What will you change in the next seven-day cycle?</li></ol>

  <h2>Practice and application</h2>
  <ol><li>Complete the Foundation Model Test 01: 30 questions, 30 marks, 15 minutes.</li><li>Read every explanation, including correct answers reached by guessing.</li><li>Choose up to five errors for immediate repair and retest them after a delay.</li></ol>
