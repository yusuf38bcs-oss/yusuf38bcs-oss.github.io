  <h2>Four subject capsules</h2>
  <div class="lbfl-info-grid">
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Physics</h3><ul><li>A vector has magnitude and direction; resolve it into perpendicular components before combining forces.</li><li>For uniform acceleration, keep the sign convention visible. The three core relations are v = u + at, s = ut + ½at² and v² = u² + 2as.</li><li>Newton's second law links net force, mass and acceleration: acceleration follows the resultant force, not one isolated force.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Chemistry</h3><ul><li>The mole is a counting unit: n = m/M and N = nNₐ.</li><li>Balance the chemical equation before using mole ratios.</li><li>A limiting reactant is the reactant consumed first; calculate both possible product amounts before deciding.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Higher Mathematics</h3><ul><li>A limit asks what a function approaches, not necessarily the value at the point.</li><li>The derivative is a rate of change and the slope of a tangent.</li><li>Begin with power, constant, sum and difference rules; record the domain or restriction when a denominator is involved.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Biology</h3><ul><li>The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.</li><li>The plasma membrane is selectively permeable; the nucleus stores most genetic material in a eukaryotic cell.</li><li>Mitochondria are associated with aerobic ATP production; ribosomes are sites of protein synthesis; Golgi bodies modify and package materials.</li></ul></section>
  </div>

  <h2>Recall without notes</h2>
  <ol><li>Define scalar and vector in one sentence and give one example of each.</li><li>Explain why an unbalanced equation makes a stoichiometric calculation unreliable.</li><li>Differentiate x³ + 4x − 7 without looking at a formula sheet.</li><li>Name the organelle that makes proteins and the organelle that modifies and packages them.</li></ol>

  <h2>Practice and application</h2>
  <ol><li>Draw a vector triangle and solve one resultant-force problem.</li><li>Balance two equations and calculate the limiting reactant in one simple example.</li><li>Solve three limits and differentiate five short functions.</li><li>Make a two-column cell-organelle table: structure and one defensible function.</li></ol>
