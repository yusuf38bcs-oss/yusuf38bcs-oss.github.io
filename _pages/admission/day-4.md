  <h2>Four subject capsules</h2>
  <div class="lbfl-info-grid">
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Physics</h3><ul><li>Centripetal acceleration points toward the centre: aᶜ = v²/r.</li><li>Gravitational force depends on the product of masses and inverse square of distance.</li><li>In circular motion, distinguish tangential speed from radial acceleration and identify the force providing the inward resultant.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Chemistry</h3><ul><li>Across a period, effective nuclear attraction generally changes atomic size and ionisation behaviour.</li><li>Ionic bonding involves electrostatic attraction after electron transfer; covalent bonding involves shared electron pairs.</li><li>Use electronegativity as a guide, not as a substitute for drawing the bonding structure.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Higher Mathematics</h3><ul><li>The tangent at x = a has slope f′(a).</li><li>Stationary points satisfy f′(x) = 0, but classification needs a change-of-sign or second-derivative argument.</li><li>State whether a point is local maximum, local minimum or neither.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Biology</h3><ul><li>A gene is a functional unit of hereditary information; an allele is an alternative form of a gene.</li><li>Genotype describes genetic constitution; phenotype is the observable expression shaped by genotype and environment.</li><li>Dominant does not mean more common, stronger or better.</li></ul></section>
  </div>

  <h2>Recall without notes</h2>
  <ol><li>What supplies centripetal force in a particular circular-motion example?</li><li>Why is a covalent bond not simply the same as an ionic bond with weaker transfer?</li><li>How do you distinguish a maximum from a minimum?</li><li>Explain dominant versus recessive without using popularity.</li></ol>

  <h2>Practice and application</h2>
  <ol><li>Complete four circular/gravitational problems and draw the force direction in each.</li><li>Predict trend direction for atomic size and ionisation energy in simple cases.</li><li>Solve two tangent questions and two maxima–minima questions.</li><li>Create a genetics vocabulary map: gene, allele, genotype, phenotype, locus.</li></ol>
