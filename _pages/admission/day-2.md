  <h2>Four subject capsules</h2>
  <div class="lbfl-info-grid">
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Physics</h3><ul><li>Draw a free-body diagram before writing ΣF = ma.</li><li>Work by a constant force is W = Fs cosθ; kinetic energy is ½mv² and gravitational potential energy near Earth is mgh.</li><li>Energy is transferred, not created by a machine. State the system and track input, output and loss.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Chemistry</h3><ul><li>Use molar mass and the balanced equation as separate steps.</li><li>For gases, distinguish pressure, volume, temperature and amount; convert units before substitution.</li><li>Percentage yield compares actual yield with theoretical yield; it is not the same as percentage purity.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Higher Mathematics</h3><ul><li>Apply d(xⁿ)/dx = nxⁿ⁻¹ and the constant, sum and difference rules.</li><li>For products and quotients, identify the rule before expanding.</li><li>A derivative can locate a stationary point; classification requires a sign or second-derivative check.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Biology</h3><ul><li>Mitosis produces genetically similar daughter cells for growth and repair.</li><li>Meiosis includes two divisions and reduces chromosome number; crossing over occurs in prophase I.</li><li>Keep chromosome, chromatid, homologous pair and sister chromatid as distinct terms.</li></ul></section>
  </div>

  <h2>Recall without notes</h2>
  <ol><li>What is the difference between net force and one applied force?</li><li>State the relationship between actual and theoretical yield.</li><li>Differentiate (2x + 1)(x² − 3) using a chosen method.</li><li>Why does meiosis support variation as well as chromosome-number reduction?</li></ol>

  <h2>Practice and application</h2>
  <ol><li>Complete one force diagram, one work–energy calculation and one conservation-of-energy explanation.</li><li>Solve two gas or stoichiometry calculations with units shown.</li><li>Differentiate one product, one quotient and one composite expression.</li><li>Draw the sequence of mitosis and meiosis with chromosome-number notes.</li></ol>
