  <h2>Four subject capsules</h2>
  <div class="lbfl-info-grid">
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Physics</h3><ul><li>Momentum p = mv is a vector quantity.</li><li>For an isolated system, total momentum is conserved even when kinetic energy is not.</li><li>Separate elastic, inelastic and perfectly inelastic descriptions; do not assume kinetic-energy conservation in every collision.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Chemistry</h3><ul><li>Atomic number identifies the element; mass number counts protons plus neutrons.</li><li>Isotopes have the same atomic number but different neutron numbers.</li><li>Electron arrangement helps explain periodic behaviour, but a shell diagram must follow the stated model and its limits.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Higher Mathematics</h3><ul><li>Differentiate before substituting a numerical point when finding a tangent slope.</li><li>Use implicit differentiation when y is mixed with x.</li><li>Check a derivative by dimensions, sign or a simple numerical estimate.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Biology</h3><ul><li>Carbohydrates commonly serve as immediate energy sources or structural materials.</li><li>Lipids are hydrophobic and include energy-storage and membrane components.</li><li>Proteins are polymers of amino acids; their function depends on sequence and three-dimensional structure.</li></ul></section>
  </div>

  <h2>Recall without notes</h2>
  <ol><li>Why can momentum be conserved when kinetic energy is not?</li><li>Define isotope without using the word mass alone.</li><li>Find dy/dx for x² + y² = 25.</li><li>Compare carbohydrate, lipid and protein by building block and one function.</li></ol>

  <h2>Practice and application</h2>
  <ol><li>Solve one recoil or collision problem and label the system boundary.</li><li>Write isotope notation for three atoms and count subatomic particles.</li><li>Complete 10 differentiation problems, including one implicit relation.</li><li>Make biomolecule flashcards with building block, bond and function.</li></ol>
