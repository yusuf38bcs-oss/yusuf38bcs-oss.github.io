  <h2>How to use the week</h2>
  <ol>
    <li><strong>Learn:</strong> read the day's concept capsule and open the linked source lesson where available.</li>
    <li><strong>Recall without notes:</strong> write or speak the key idea before checking the prompt.</li>
    <li><strong>Solve:</strong> complete the day's questions or written work under a timer.</li>
    <li><strong>Explain:</strong> state why the valid answer works and why the tempting alternative fails.</li>
    <li><strong>Repair:</strong> return to the linked source concept and record the misconception.</li>
    <li><strong>Retest:</strong> revisit the error on Day 3, Day 6 or the weekly test.</li>
  </ol>

  <h2>Study contract</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Use a timer, but do not trade reasoning for speed.</li>
    <li>Keep an error log with three fields: misconception, source concept, retest date.</li>
    <li>Do not treat a score as a diagnosis of ability; use it as a signal for the next repair action.</li>
    <li>Where a topic has no existing LBFL source route yet, use the day's capsule as a bounded foundation and mark the gap for later curriculum development.</li>
