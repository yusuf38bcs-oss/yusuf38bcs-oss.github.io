  <h2>Four subject capsules</h2>
  <div class="lbfl-info-grid">
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Physics</h3><ul><li>Choose a representation before calculation: force diagram, energy path, momentum system or motion graph.</li><li>A multi-step problem is a chain of justified smaller claims; write the governing principle beside each step.</li><li>Check units, limiting cases and whether the final direction makes physical sense.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Chemistry</h3><ul><li>VSEPR predicts approximate electron-domain arrangement from repulsion; lone pairs affect molecular shape.</li><li>Hybridization is a model for orbital mixing used to describe bonding patterns; state the model rather than treating it as a visible object.</li><li>Distinguish molecular shape from electron-pair geometry.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Higher Mathematics</h3><ul><li>Translate a word problem into a function, identify the variable and state the domain.</li><li>Differentiate, solve the stationary condition, test classification and interpret the result.</li><li>In past-question practice, classify the method before doing algebra.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="lbfl-info-card"><h3>Biology</h3><ul><li>DNA stores hereditary information in a double-stranded structure with complementary base pairing.</li><li>RNA is usually single-stranded and participates in information transfer or protein synthesis in different forms.</li><li>Replication, transcription and translation are related but distinct processes.</li></ul></section>
  </div>

  <h2>Recall without notes</h2>
  <ol><li>Which principle would you select first for a ramp-and-collision problem, and why?</li><li>What is the difference between shape and electron-pair geometry?</li><li>List the four stages of a calculus optimisation solution.</li><li>Compare DNA and RNA by sugar, strand tendency and one role.</li></ol>

  <h2>Practice and application</h2>
  <ol><li>Solve one mechanics question in two methods and compare the assumptions.</li><li>Draw Lewis structures for simple molecules, then state shape and hybridization model.</li><li>Do a small set of calculus past-question-style problems with method labels.</li><li>Create a DNA–RNA comparison and sequence map.</li></ol>
