# Life Practices and Physiology

This concept page connects physiology, behaviour, learning discipline and practical life application without exposing draft architecture notes.

## Core Idea

Biology becomes meaningful when learners can connect body systems with real choices, habits and behaviour. Physiology explains how breathing, circulation, digestion, nervous control, endocrine feedback and stress responses support daily life.
