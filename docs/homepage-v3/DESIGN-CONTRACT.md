## Locked design rules
- Scientific × Editorial × Bengali × Contemporary.
- Deep teal/navy + cream with restrained gold/mint/aqua.
- Premium means quieter hierarchy, stronger typography, integrated scientific imagery, lower decorative noise, and smoother tonal section transitions.
- Gold is a scarce emphasis signal; teal/mint communicate biology and progression.
- Mobile header remains 64–68px; every authored interactive target remains at least 44×44px.
- Hero remains eyebrow → H1 → brand line → short proposition → CTAs → compact 4-step cycle.

- Full Observe/Question/Connect/Explain explanation remains in Synaptic Bridge, not repeated in the hero.
- Four pathways remain visible; mobile remains a single column, not swipe-only.
- Evidence retains compact accountability rather than a dominant personal profile.
- Motion is restrained, non-essential, and disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Save-Data behavior remains request-conscious.
