## Scientific checks

- Chloroplast identified as a plastid specialized for photosynthesis.
- Double-membrane envelope, stroma, thylakoid membrane, lumen, grana and stroma lamellae are distinguished.
- Photosynthetic electron transport is placed on thylakoid membrane; carbon fixation is placed in stroma.
- Chloroplast own DNA/ribosomal machinery is presented as semiautonomy, not full independence.
- Chloroplast, chromoplast and leucoplast are distinguished without claiming all plastids are photosynthetic.
- Chloroplast–mitochondrion comparison preserves the distinct thylakoid versus crista/inner-membrane systems.
