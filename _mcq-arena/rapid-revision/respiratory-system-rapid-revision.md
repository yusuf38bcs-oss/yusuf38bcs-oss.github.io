# Respiratory System Rapid Revision
## শ্বসনতন্ত্র দ্রুত রিভিশন: Ventilation, Gas Exchange & Transport

<div class="notice--success" markdown="1">

**Use this before MCQ practice.**

## 1. One-line map / এক লাইনের পথচিত্র

```text
Nose -> Pharynx -> Larynx -> Trachea -> Bronchi -> Bronchioles -> Alveoli
```

**Core idea:** Respiration is not only breathing. It includes ventilation, gas exchange, gas transport and cellular use of oxygen.

**বাংলা ধারণা:** Respiration শুধু শ্বাস-প্রশ্বাস নয়; air movement, alveolar gas exchange, blood transport এবং cell-level energy production—সব মিলেই respiratory function.

---

## 2. Ventilation vs Gas Exchange

| Term | Meaning | বাংলা অর্থ |
|---|---|---|
| Ventilation | Air movement in and out of lungs | lungs-এ air ঢোকা-বের হওয়া |
| Gas exchange | O₂ and CO₂ diffusion | O₂/CO₂ diffusion |
| Inspiration | Air enters lungs | বাতাস lungs-এ ঢোকে |
| Expiration | Air leaves lungs | বাতাস lungs থেকে বের হয় |

**Exam trap:** Ventilation and gas exchange are not the same.

---

## 3. Diaphragm and pressure logic

| Event | Thoracic volume | Pressure | Air movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diaphragm contracts | Increases | Decreases | Air enters |
| Diaphragm relaxes | Decreases | Increases | Air leaves |

**বাংলা memory:** Volume বাড়লে pressure কমে, air ঢোকে। Volume কমলে pressure বাড়ে, air বের হয়।

---

## 4. Alveoli: gas exchange surface

Alveoli are efficient because they have:

- Thin wall — পাতলা wall
- Large surface area — বড় surface area
- Rich capillary network — প্রচুর capillary
- Short diffusion distance — diffusion distance কম
- Moist lining — moist surface

**Memory:** Thin + wide + blood-rich = fast gas exchange.

---

## 5. Surfactant

Surfactant reduces alveolar surface tension.

**বাংলা:** Surfactant alveoli-এর surface tension কমায়, ফলে alveoli collapse হওয়ার ঝুঁকি কমে।

**Exam trap:** Surfactant is not a respiratory gas. It is a protective surface-active substance.

---

## 6. Oxygen transport

Most oxygen travels as **oxyhemoglobin**.

```text
O2 + Hb -> HbO2
```

**বাংলা:** অধিকাংশ O₂ hemoglobin-এর সাথে যুক্ত হয়ে transport হয়। অল্প অংশ plasma-তে dissolved অবস্থায় থাকে।

---

## 7. Carbon dioxide transport

CO₂ is transported mainly as:

1. Bicarbonate ion — HCO₃⁻
2. Carbaminohemoglobin
3. Dissolved CO₂ in plasma

**বাংলা memory:** CO₂-এর প্রধান transport form হলো bicarbonate.

---

## 8. High-yield MCQ traps

| Trap | Correct idea | বাংলা সংশোধন |
|---|---|---|
| Ventilation = gas exchange | False | Ventilation air movement |
| Surfactant carries oxygen | False | Surfactant surface tension কমায় |
| Diaphragm relaxes during inspiration | False | Inspiration-এ diaphragm contracts |
| CO₂ mostly dissolved in plasma | False | CO₂ mostly bicarbonate |
| Alveoli has thick wall | False | Alveoli wall thin |

---

## 9. Respiratory disorders quick view

| Disorder | Core idea | বাংলা cue |
|---|---|---|
| COPD | Chronic airflow limitation | দীর্ঘমেয়াদি airflow obstruction |
| Emphysema | Alveolar wall damage | gas exchange surface কমে যায় |
| Bronchitis | Bronchial inflammation | mucus/cough/airway issue |
| Asthma | Reversible airway narrowing | bronchoconstriction |

---

## 10. Rapid self-test

1. Main muscle of inspiration?  
**Answer:** Diaphragm. / **উত্তর:** Diaphragm.

2. Main site of gas exchange?  
**Answer:** Alveoli. / **উত্তর:** Alveoli.

3. What prevents alveolar collapse?  
**Answer:** Surfactant. / **উত্তর:** Surfactant.

4. Main carrier of oxygen?  
**Answer:** Hemoglobin. / **উত্তর:** Hemoglobin.

5. Main form of CO₂ transport?  
**Answer:** Bicarbonate. / **উত্তর:** Bicarbonate ion.
