# Digestive System Rapid Revision
## Enzyme, Bile, Liver, Pancreas & Absorption

<div class="notice--success" markdown="1">

**Use this before MCQ practice.**  
Read this page in 5 minutes, then solve the Digestive System MCQ Set 01.

</div>

## 1. One-line map

```text
Food -> Mouth -> Oesophagus -> Stomach -> Duodenum -> Small intestine -> Large intestine
```

**Core idea:** Digestion means large food molecules are broken into absorbable molecules by **mechanical action**, **enzyme action**, **bile-assisted emulsification**, and **intestinal absorption**.

---

## 2. Gastric juice at a glance

| Component | Source | Main function |
|---|---|---|
| HCl | Parietal cell | Acidic pH, pepsin activation |
| Pepsinogen | Chief/peptic cell | Inactive form of pepsin |
| Mucus | Mucous cell | Protects stomach wall |
| Intrinsic factor | Parietal cell | Helps vitamin B12 absorption |

**Exam trap:** Pepsin works in acidic medium; trypsin works in alkaline intestinal medium.

---

## 3. Bile at a glance

Bile is not an enzyme.

Bile helps by:

1. **Fat emulsification**
2. Helping absorption of **vitamin A, D, E, K**
3. Carrying some waste products through bile route

**Memory:** Bile = fat helper, not fat enzyme.

---

## 4. Pancreas at a glance

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Wirsung duct | Main pancreatic duct |
| Pancreatic juice | Enzyme-rich alkaline digestive juice |
| Trypsin | Protein digestion in small intestine |
| Amylase | Carbohydrate digestion |
| Lipase | Fat digestion |

**Exam trap:** Wirsung duct belongs to pancreas, not pharynx or liver.

---

## 5. Liver at a glance

Liver is not only a digestive accessory organ. It is also a metabolic center.

Important functions:

- Bile formation
- Glycogen storage
- Gluconeogenesis
- Detoxification
- Plasma protein synthesis
- Kupffer cell-mediated phagocytosis

**Kupffer cell:** liver macrophage.

---

## 6. Absorption at a glance

Small intestine is the main site of absorption because it has:

- Long length
- Villi
- Microvilli
- Rich blood supply
- Lacteals for fat absorption

Transport methods:

```text
Diffusion + Facilitated diffusion + Active transport + Secondary active transport
```

---

## 7. Enzyme matching table

| Enzyme | Substrate | Product idea |
|---|---|---|
| Pepsin | Protein | Peptides/peptone |
| Rennin | Casein | Paracasein |
| Lactase | Lactose | Glucose + galactose |
| Trypsin | Protein/peptide | Smaller peptides |
| Lipase | Fat | Fatty acid + glycerol |
| Amylase | Starch | Maltose/smaller carbohydrate |

---

## 8. High-yield MCQ traps

| Trap | Correct idea |
|---|---|
| Bile is an enzyme | False. Bile emulsifies fat. |
| Lactase digests protein | False. Lactase digests lactose. |
| Pepsin works in alkaline medium | False. Pepsin works in acidic medium. |
| Wirsung duct is in pharynx | False. It is pancreatic duct. |
| Kupffer cell is in brain | False. It is liver macrophage. |

---

## 9. Rapid self-test

1. Which enzyme works best in acidic medium?  
**Answer:** Pepsin.

2. Which duct carries pancreatic juice?  
**Answer:** Wirsung duct / pancreatic duct.

3. Which cell produces HCl?  
**Answer:** Parietal cell.

4. Which vitamins need bile-assisted fat absorption?  
**Answer:** A, D, E, K.

5. What is the main absorption organ?  
**Answer:** Small intestine.

---

## Final Memory Card

```text
Acidic stomach -> Pepsin
Bile -> Fat emulsification
Pancreas -> Wirsung duct
Liver -> Kupffer cell + metabolism
Small intestine -> Absorption
```
