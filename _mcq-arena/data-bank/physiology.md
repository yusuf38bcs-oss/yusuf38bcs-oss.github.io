    - c: Salivary amylase
    - d: Trypsin
    correct_key: c
    explanation: Salivary amylase (ptyalin) begins carbohydrate digestion by hydrolyzing

    - a: Blood glucose
    - b: Blood pressure
    - c: Body temperature
    - d: Respiratory rate
    correct_key: b
    explanation: Baroreceptors located in the carotid sinus and aortic arch detect
      changes in arterial blood pressure and trigger autonomic responses to restore
      homeostasis.
