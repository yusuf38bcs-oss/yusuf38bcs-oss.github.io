# Genetics & Laws of Inheritance MCQ Bank

This page provides concept-based MCQ practice for Mendelian inheritance, chromosome behaviour, alleles, genotype, phenotype, dominance, segregation, independent assortment, linkage, mutation, molecular inheritance, and genetic probability.

## Preparation checklist

Revise Mendel's laws, monohybrid and dihybrid crosses, test cross, incomplete dominance, codominance, multiple alleles, sex-linked inheritance, pedigree logic, DNA structure, replication, transcription, translation, mutation, and chromosome-based inheritance.

## Reasoning method

Genetics MCQs often require calculation and conceptual filtering. Identify the cross, write the genotype logic, decide whether the trait follows Mendelian or non-Mendelian inheritance, and then select the option that matches the predicted ratio or mechanism.
