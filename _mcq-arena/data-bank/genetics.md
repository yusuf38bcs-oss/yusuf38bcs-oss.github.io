      genetic inheritance interactions?
    options:
    - a: 9:3:3:1
    - b: '12:3:1'
    - c: '9:7'
    - d: '15:1'
    correct_key: b
    explanation: Dominant epistasis occurs when a dominant allele at one locus masks

  - id: q2
    question_text: Which enzyme relieves torsional strain ahead of the replication
      fork?
    options:
    - a: DNA Polymerase III
    - b: RNA Primase
    - c: DNA Topoisomerase (Gyrase)
    - d: DNA Ligase
    correct_key: c
    explanation: Topoisomerases introduce temporary breaks into the DNA backbone to
      relieve supercoiling tension generated by helicase unwinding.
  - id: q3
    question_text: What is the primary function of the enzyme DNA ligase during DNA
      replication?
    options:
    - a: Synthesizing RNA primers
    - b: Unwinding the DNA double helix
    - c: Joining Okazaki fragments on the lagging strand
    - d: Proofreading newly synthesized DNA
    correct_key: c
    explanation: DNA ligase catalyzes the formation of phosphodiester bonds between
      adjacent DNA fragments, specifically sealing the nicks between Okazaki fragments
      on the lagging strand.
