  - id: q1
    question_text: Which organelle is responsible for photosynthesis in plant cells?
    options:
    - a: Mitochondria
    - b: Chloroplast
    - c: Ribosome
    - d: Peroxisome
    correct_key: b
    explanation: Chloroplasts contain chlorophyll and the thylakoid membrane system
      where the light-dependent reactions of photosynthesis occur.
  - id: q2
    question_text: Which structure maintains turgor pressure and provides structural
      support in plant cells?
    options:
    - a: Cell membrane
    - b: Central vacuole
    - c: Nucleus
    - d: Golgi apparatus
    correct_key: b
    explanation: The central vacuole in plant cells stores water and solutes; when
      full, it exerts turgor pressure against the cell wall, maintaining rigidity.
  - id: q3
    question_text: Which cell organelle is known as the 'powerhouse of the cell' due
      to ATP production?
    options:
    - a: Nucleus
    - b: Chloroplast
    - c: Mitochondria
    - d: Lysosome
    correct_key: c
    explanation: Mitochondria generate ATP through cellular respiration, earning them
      the title 'powerhouse of the cell' across both plant and animal kingdoms.
