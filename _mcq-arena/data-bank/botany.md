# Cell Organelles & Plant Biology MCQ Bank

This page provides focused MCQ practice for cell organelles, plant-cell structure, and foundational plant biology. The questions are designed to test whether learners can connect structure with function, identify organelles from clues, and avoid common misconceptions about plant-cell organization.

## Before you begin

Revise nucleus, chromosome, mitochondria, plastid, ribosome, endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi body, vacuole, cell wall, plasma membrane, and basic plant tissue ideas. Pay attention to functional keywords such as photosynthesis, protein synthesis, respiration, storage, secretion, and cellular control.

## Practice method

Answer each item first without looking at the explanation. After submission, read the answer explanation and write the concept behind the correct answer in one sentence. This will convert option-selection practice into real biology learning.
